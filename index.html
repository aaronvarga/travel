How these scores work: Budget is weighted double against your $12k target / $15k strongly preferred maximum; Weather, Swim, Variety, Ease, Food, Risk, Nights, and Novelty are each rated 1-5 once. Total is out of 50 (PTO is tracked as data but not part of the default total). Nights rewards trip length: 12+ nights → 5, 11 → 4, 10 → 3, 9 → 2, 8 or fewer → 1 — more vacation for the same overhead. Card order follows this table; default ties prefer a range within the budget preference, then fewer PTO days, then lower budget ceiling and floor. Cost never removes an itinerary. Plan Health-Check

Risks worth keeping visible

  • The bias is retired. The Portugal/Italy preference no longer touches the ranking. The data says Portugal first at 40, Madeira + Mallorca second at 37, Portugal + Sicily third at 35, and Iceland fourth at 35; genuine warm water only shows up at Turkey's sea and Hawaii.
  • Hybrids resolve the core tension. All five hybrids keep a Portugal opener and hand the swimming to warm water elsewhere. Madeira openers buy epic hiking at the cost of extra flight legs and FNC wind risk; mainland openers stay calm and cheap. Crete and Sicily legs feed the Italy/Greece bias but press the preferred maximum; the newest hybrid, Madeira + Mallorca, is the only one that keeps the Madeira hiking week and stays within-preference.
  • Madeira runs on gates and buffers. Every Madeira variant needs verified ticket protection or deliberately buffered separate tickets, the Lisbon buffer night before the onward hop, and a hard family-airfare gate. The Sicily variants also rely on a thin Lisbon–Palermo market whose exact 2027 operating dates remain unconfirmed.
  • Italy drifted expensive. The Salento-Maratea-Amalfi file now reads ~$11.4k-16.3k realistic, with a preference-fit trim around ~$12.4k-14.8k. It is an excluded stretch reference, not a budget-safe pick.
  • Hawaii's own rollup crosses the preferred maximum. Hawaii's line-item budget table sums to ~$11k–15.8k with insurance. Combined with only nine hotel nights and a been-there Maui factor, it sits #8 before complete quotes land.
  • Maui + Kauai is the premium scenery benchmark. It fixes Hawaii's length penalty with 12 nights and adds Nā Pali, Waimea and Hanalei, but Maui is a repeat, Hāʻena/boat days are fragile, and the reconciled $14.6k–21.6k band places it #13 despite the same 32/50 raw score.
  • Turkey is a warm-water wild card, not the automatic winner. The Turkish Riviera has the warmest non-Hawaii sea on the board, but June heat, long-haul airfare, the scoped advisory, and a reconciled high case near $17.4k keep it an excluded reference at 33/50.
  • Croatia is excluded outright. Croatia scores 35/50 at the best warm-water price, but the family did Croatia in 2026 and this route repeats Split/Hvar/Korčula. Excluded from the recommendation by explicit decision; the row stays on the scoreboard for reference.
  • The Pittsburgh commitment drives the date math. Returning on 6/23/2027 is valid and preferred; every plan must keep the family in Pittsburgh for the full days 6/24/2027–6/26/2027. Some plans still use 6/9/2027–6/22/2027 for schedule or price reasons, while Balkans and California move into early July.
  • Complexity still matters. Sardinia/Corsica, Sicily/Malta, Greece via Lisbon, and the Balkans all offer major variety, but each adds either ferry, cross-border, flight-chain, or four-base friction. Balkans also picked up July peak-pricing risk.
