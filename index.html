Plan Health-Check

Risks worth keeping visible

Preference now matters

Portugal is the favorite so far, so it should not sit below technically stronger but less wanted options. The cold-water caveat is the only reason it is not an uncontested pick.

Hybrids resolve the core tension

Portugal + Sicily and Portugal + Crete keep the loved Portugal opener and fix its cold water. The price is real: an extra intra-trip flight, more bases (4 for Sicily, 3 for Crete), and a tighter cap. They are the strongest fit to the Portugal-then-Italy-then-Greece bias if the family accepts that added logistics.

Italy drifted expensive

The Salento-Maratea-Amalfi file now reads ~$13.2k-16.4k realistic, with a cap-fit trim around ~$12.4k-14.8k. It is a stretch finalist, not a budget-safe pick.

Turkey is airfare-gated

The Turkish Riviera has excellent warm-water fit, but the current planning band reaches ~$15.9k. It belongs high only if AYT/DLM flights price cleanly.

Croatia is proven, not fresh

Croatia remains one of the best Europe values, but doing it right after last year's loved Croatia trip weakens the novelty and variety case.

Blackout drives the date math

Jun 23-26 is now protected across the dashboard. Most Europe plans use Jun 9-22; Balkans and California move into early July to avoid the Pittsburgh conflict cleanly.

Complexity still matters

Sardinia/Corsica, Sicily/Malta, Greece via Lisbon, and the Balkans all offer major variety, but each adds either ferry, cross-border, flight-chain, or four-base friction. Balkans also picked up July peak-pricing risk.
