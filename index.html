Category Winners

What wins each decision

Overall recommendation

Madeira + Mallorca — top score (39/50): Madeira ridge hiking plus a sandy Mediterranean swim in Mallorca, and the only hybrid that stays cap-clean end to end. Portugal and Iceland tie for the runner-up score at 38; Portugal + Sicily, Portugal + Crete, and Greece via Lisbon tie just behind at 37.

Best of both worlds

Madeira + Mallorca is now the cleanest answer: Madeira hiking plus a warm swim, no Crete/Sicily leg needed, and cap-clean. Portugal + Crete is the calmer, mainland-opener alternative if the Madeira ridge week isn't a must-have. Madeira + Crete adds the Stairway to Heaven week but now breaches the cap.

Epic scenery + hiking

Madeira + Mallorca — the same Arieiro-above-the-clouds, 25 Fontes, Porto Moniz week, paired with a Mediterranean swim that keeps the whole trip under the $15k cap. Madeira + Crete and Madeira + Sicily offer the same hiking but both now breach the cap.

Pure score

Madeira + Mallorca at 39/50. Hawaii, the original pure-score winner, sits at #13 at 33/50: an honest budget band (~$11k–15.8k) that breaches the cap, only 9 hotel nights where every Europe route delivers 12, and a been-there Maui factor (novelty 3).

Best Italy route

Italy: Salento-Amalfi because it has the best food/variety stretch, even after the budget drift made it a cap-fit trim decision.

Warm-water Europe

Turkish Riviera has the warmest non-Hawaii sea on the board (75–79°F) and a clean three-base AYT-to-DLM route — but brutal June air heat (Antalya 87–95°F) crashes its overall score to #16/last. Pick it only if the heat is an acceptable tradeoff for the warmest Europe-style swim.

Best Europe value

Croatia wins value on paper ($8.6k–12.8k) but is excluded — 2026 repeat, novelty 1/5. Greece via Lisbon ($9.9k–15.6k) has the next-lowest floor, though its ceiling now also breaches the cap.

Best food

Italy: Salento-Amalfi edges Sicily/Malta once route simplicity and family preference are considered.

Best domestic

California Pacific Coast would be the pick for passport-free mainland driving, but it is excluded by family decision (kept for reference); no ranked trip fills the road-trip niche.

Most adventurous

Balkans has the biggest adventure arc but is excluded by family decision (kept for reference); Turkish Riviera is the cleanest ranked adventure-lite alternative.

Plan Health-Check

Risks worth keeping visible

  • The bias is retired. The Portugal/Italy preference no longer touches the ranking — under the old order it was worth up to ten rank positions (Madeira + Sicily sat #5 despite the board's worst risk score). Hearts still say Portugal/Italy; the data says Madeira + Mallorca first, then Portugal and Iceland tied for the runner-up score at 38, with Portugal + Sicily, Portugal + Crete, and Greece via Lisbon tied just behind at 37 — all still June-Med swim 3, since genuine warm water only shows up at Turkey's sea and Hawaii.
  • Hybrids resolve the core tension. All five hybrids keep a Portugal opener and hand the swimming to warm water elsewhere. Madeira openers buy epic hiking at the cost of extra flight legs and FNC wind risk; mainland openers stay calm and cheap. Crete and Sicily legs feed the Italy/Greece bias but press the cap; the newest hybrid, Madeira + Mallorca, is the only one that keeps the Madeira hiking week and stays cap-clean.
  • Madeira runs on gates and buffers. Every Madeira variant only works as designed: one protected ticket, the Lisbon buffer night before the onward hop (FNC wind delays peak in summer), and a hard family-airfare gate. The Sicily variants also ride a thinned-out air map: Ryanair exited Lisbon–Palermo in Nov 2024, leaving an easyJet monopoly with no low-cost floor — though Catania does carry a seasonal Delta JFK nonstop (Apr–Oct), so the fragile leg is the Lisbon–Palermo fare, not the US connection.
  • Italy drifted expensive. The Salento-Maratea-Amalfi file now reads ~$13.2k-16.4k realistic, with a cap-fit trim around ~$12.4k-14.8k. It is a stretch finalist, not a budget-safe pick.
  • Hawaii's own rollup breaches the cap. The scoreboard used to say $10.6k–14.4k; Hawaii's line-item budget table sums to ~$11k–15.8k with insurance. Combined with only 9 hotel nights and a been-there Maui factor, it now sits #13 even before quotes land.
  • Turkey's heat crashes it to last. The Turkish Riviera has the warmest non-Hawaii sea on the board (~77°F) — but June air is brutal, with Antalya running 87–95°F. That drops the Weather score to 3 and sinks the trip to #16/last at 32/50, tied on score with Madeira + Sicily but losing the tie-break. Airfare risk (planning band reaches ~$15.9k) is a secondary concern next to the heat.
  • Croatia is excluded outright. Croatia scores 35/50 at the best warm-water price, but the family did Croatia in 2026 and this route repeats Split/Hvar/Korčula. Excluded from the recommendation by explicit decision; the row stays on the scoreboard for reference.
  • Blackout drives the date math. Jun 23-26 is now protected across the dashboard. Most Europe plans use Jun 9-22; Balkans and California move into early July to avoid the Pittsburgh conflict cleanly.
  • Complexity still matters. Sardinia/Corsica, Sicily/Malta, Greece via Lisbon, and the Balkans all offer major variety, but each adds either ferry, cross-border, flight-chain, or four-base friction. Balkans also picked up July peak-pricing risk.

Options Considered

The "Madeira-variety" exploration — combos we weighed

Every epic-hiking / swim-variety combo we discussed, and why most weren't built

Sparked by Madeira's mid-trip flight friction plus wanting a leg with epic hiking and dramatically different scenery and good swimming — while staying novel against the Mediterranean-beach board and our recent Mexico trip. Captured here so we don't lose the thread.

Built / in progress

  • Canary Islands (Tenerife + La Palma) — built, ranked #12 (33/50) Madeira's ecological twin (Macaronesian laurel forest + Teide volcano) — but flight connectivity is the tradeoff, not the upgrade: Pittsburgh to Tenerife/La Palma typically runs three connections via Madrid, versus Madeira's single-stop United EWR–FNC nonstop.
  • Albania (Accursed Mountains: Theth/Valbona + the Riviera: Ksamil) — built, scores highest (44/50), excluded by family decision The best warm-sea + novel pick: alpine drama + Caribbean-clear swimming, cheap, one-country ground transport, no island-hop trap.
  • Madeira + Mallorca — built, new #1 (39/50) The Madeira ridge-hiking week paired with a sandy Mediterranean swim in Mallorca instead of a second Atlantic or Greek leg — the only hybrid that stays cap-clean end to end.
  • Dolomites + Venice + Sardinia — built, ranked #11 (34/50) Best pure epic-hiking (Tre Cime, Seceda) with a Venice opener and an epic-beach Sardinia finale. Landlocked hiking half = the variety.
  • Iceland (Reykjavík + Golden Circle + South Coast + Jökulsárlón) — built, #3 on the data, tied with Portugal for the runner-up score (38/50) The most novel-looking option — nothing like the Med board or Mexico. Swim = warm geothermal lagoons, not the (cold) sea.

Prompts drafted, not yet built

  • Crete — add epic hiking (Samaria Gorge, Imbros, Lefka Ori) — prompt ready Not a new trip — an enhancement to the existing Crete legs so Crete alone carries the Madeira mountain-vs-sea variety with zero Atlantic flight.

Considered, set aside

  • Costa Rica (Arenal + Monteverde + Manuel Antonio) — off the table A complete plan already existed, but it photographs like Yucatán/Riviera Maya — no novelty right after a Mexico trip. The look is structural (beach + jungle), so a rebuild wouldn't fix it.
  • Slovenia (Julian Alps + Soča + Bled) — not built Genuinely novel emerald-alpine look and cheap, but the swim is turquoise river/glacial lakes, not warm sea.
  • Swiss Bernese Oberland (Lauterbrunnen + glacial lakes) — not built Epic and unique, but pushes hard past the $15k cap — the likely budget-breaker.
  • Greek mainland (Zagori/Vikos + Pelion) — not built Deepest-gorge + stone-village hiking is novel, but it's still Greece (we have Crete) and the Aegean beaches read Med.
  • Montenegro (Durmitor + Bay of Kotor) — not built Epic canyon + warm sea, but likely overlaps the existing Balkans road-trip and the coast looks Med.
  • Azores — rejected Same via-Lisbon Atlantic bottleneck as Madeira — doesn't fix the connectivity problem that started all this.

Also noted along the way: Corsica (already on the board inside Sardinia + Corsica) and Crete are the closest "Madeira mountain-vs-sea substitute you may already own." The recurring tension: Albania took the best warm-sea-and-novel slot, so what's left trades warm ocean (Iceland/Slovenia/Switzerland = lagoon/river/lake swim) or partly echoes the Med board (Greece/Montenegro).
