Category Winners

What wins each decision

Best overall

Costa Rica because it is the only trip that is simultaneously cheap, warm-water, nature-heavy, varied, and efficient.

Best Europe

Croatia because it has the best Europe budget and a cleaner family route than the higher-drama Mediterranean options.

Best new addition

Italy: Salento-Amalfi because it adds the strongest food, warm-water, and culture mix of the three new plans.

Best swim

Costa Rica still wins outright. Hawaii is second; Italy, Sardinia/Corsica, Sicily/Malta, and the Balkans are the best warm-water Europe-style options.

Best food

Sicily & Malta remains the safest picky-eater food route. Italy is the closest challenger.

Best domestic

California Pacific Coast is the best mainland no-passport road trip, but it is not budget-ready in the current version.

Most adventurous

Balkans has the biggest wow/adventure arc, but the rental-car and border friction make it a poor first choice for this family brief.

Best fallback

Portugal is still the calm, known, value-forward fallback if cool Atlantic water is acceptable.

Plan Health-Check

Risks worth keeping visible

Best option stayed stable

Adding Italy, California, and the Balkans did not change the top call. Costa Rica still has the rare combination of low cost, warm water, high variety, and simple enough logistics.

Two additions break the cap

California's current high end is ~$17.5k and Balkans is ~$15.2k. Both need trimming before they can compete under the $15k hard cap.

Italy is good but tight

Italy scores well on weather, water, food, and variety, but the budget lands at ~$12k-15k. Treat it as a stretch finalist only if flights and automatic car pricing come in clean.

Croatia remains the Europe answer

Italy and Sardinia have warmer/foodier strengths, but Croatia has the best Europe budget, a strong route, and enough swim/town balance to win the Europe category.

Domestic does not mean cheap

California is administratively easy, but coastal lodging, one-way car pricing, parking, major tickets, and cold-water compensation make it expensive.

Adventure has a cost

The Balkans route is visually strong and water-heavy, but cross-border rental permissions, Albania drop-off, Saranda-Corfu ferry timing, and the 7.5-8 hour Kotor-Ksamil drive are real family-friction points.
