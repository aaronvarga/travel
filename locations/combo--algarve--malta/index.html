Est. ~$140 · dinner out + fuel/tolls

The big mainland drive: Cascais → Carvoeiro is ~285 km / 2h58 before stops. Do not stack a major activity unless everyone is in great shape.

Carvoeiro

PhotosIG · CarvoeiroFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Beach free (lifeguarded summer). Town parking meters ~$1/hr. Benagil–Marinha boat: $35 adult / $20 child 3–10.

June weather

75°F day / 63°F night, ~1 rainy day. Sea 65°F (59–73°F) — cool but swimmable.

Save Book the Benagil boat online in advance — kids 3–10 are $20 vs $35; family of 4 ~$115 not $140.
Splurge Private 2-hr Benagil cave boat charter, ~$165–275 for the boat — go early, at your own pace.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Mama Mia — Italian pizza/pasta, ~$11–15 4.1★ (269 rev) — thin-crust pizza + pasta; 3-course set ~$20; tiny, book ahead.
  • Levels Carvoeiro — International/grill, ~$13–20 — explicit kids' plates: mini burgers + 'chicken pops'/fries.
  • Jota Lita — Portuguese family-run (est. 1988), ~$11–15 — does plain burgers for kids, relaxed.
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Praia da Marinha ↗ — One of Europe's most famous beaches, ~15 min drive — dramatic arches, clearer & quieter.
    PhotosIG · Praia da MarinhaFlickr
  • Alvor boardwalk ↗ — flat lagoon walk and town snacks when cliffs or boat timing are not working.
    PhotosIG · Alvor boardwalkFlickr
  • Praia de Vale Centeanes ↗ — cliff-backed sandy beach near Carvoeiro with easier logistics than Marinha.
    PhotosIG · Praia de Vale CenteanesFlickr

Ferragudo

PhotosIG · FerragudoFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Village + Praia Grande beach free. Small free car parks (limited; arrive early). No entry gates.

June weather

75°F day / 63°F night, ~1 rainy day. Sea 65°F (59–73°F) — cool but swimmable.

Lagos

PhotosIG · LagosFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Town + marina free (free playground at north end). Power-boat cave cruise from $20 adult/$15 child; catamaran $55/$40.

June weather

75°F day / 63°F night, ~1 rainy day. Sea 65°F (59–73°F) — cool but swimmable.

Save Take the $20/$15 power-boat grotto cruise not the $55/$40 catamaran — same scenery, family saves ~$110.

Slide & Splash

PhotosIG · Slide & SplashFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

2026: adult (11–64) $35 low/$40 high; junior (≥1m, ≤10) $25/$30; baby <1m free. Online = 10% off. Family (2+2) high season ≈ $135 gate / $120 online. Open Apr–Oct.

June weather

75°F day / 63°F night, ~1 rainy day. Sea 65°F (59–73°F) — cool but swimmable.

Save Buy online for 10% off (~$4/adult) — family saves ~$13–15 and skips the ticket queue. BYO picnic allowed.
Splurge Rent a shaded sunbed/gazebo for the day (~$15–35) so parents have a home base.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • On-site self-service — Buffet + snack bars: burgers, chicken/nuggets, chips, pizza, ice cream — standard picky-kid fare. No re-entry.
  • BYO picnic — Bringing your own food is allowed — caps the day's total.

Blue Lagoon + Comino

PhotosIG · Blue LagoonIG · CominoFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

The 2026 visitor system requires a free pre-booked Blue Lagoon pass to step on land, with morning/afternoon/evening slots; commercial boat trips commonly ~€30–45 adult / child discounts.

June weather

Air ~80–84°F / 69–71°F. Comino water should be around 73–74°F on 6/21/2027, excellent compared with Portugal and usually calmer/warmer-feeling inside the lagoon.

Save Book the free slot yourself before buying a tour; choose the morning slot for clearer water and fewer boats.
Splurge Private or semi-private Comino/Gozo boat, ~€250–450 family, avoids packed party boats.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • I Pupi Pizzeria Valletta — excellent-value pizza, 4.9★, ~$12–20 pp
  • Casa Sotto — Roman pizza in Valletta, top family pizza pick
  • Sally Port Pizzeria — pizza/pasta in Valletta, 4.8★; familiar dinner fallback
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Għajn Tuffieħa ↗ — Malta beach day with sand and no Comino boat logistics.
  • Ghajn Tuffieha ↗ — proper sandy beach day without Comino boat logistics.
  • Ramla Bay ↗ — red-sand beach version of a Gozo day if a full island loop is too much.

Gozo day trip

PhotosIG · GozoFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Gozo Channel return foot passenger €4.65 adult; children 3–12 €1.15; pay on return from Gozo. Fast ferry from Valletta is more, about €8.50 one way.

June weather

Air ~80–84°F / 69–71°F. Xlendi/Ramla water should be around 73–75°F on 6/22/2027.

Save Go as foot passengers and use a Gozo taxi/Bolt for key stops; taking a Malta rental car over adds cost and parking friction.
