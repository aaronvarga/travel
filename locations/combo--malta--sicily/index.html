Est. ~$360–620 · flight + transfers + dinner

SleepValletta / Sliema · night 1 of 3

Skip the rental car: left-side driving and tight parking make a short Malta stay easier with ferries, taxis, and tours.

Valletta old town

PhotosIG · VallettaFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

St John’s Co-Cathedral: open Mon–Sat 9:00–16:45, last admission 16:00; adults usually ~€15, children/student concessions. Grand Master’s Palace: €12 adult / €10 youth / €8 child 6–11; Throne Room closed until further notice.

June weather

Air ~80–84°F / 69–71°F. Malta/Comino sea should be around 73–74°F on 6/20/2027–6/22/2027, with sheltered lagoons often feeling warmer.

Save Use Valletta ferries/ride-hail instead of a Malta rental car; left-side driving and parking do not help a 3-night stay.
Splurge A harbor cruise or private dgħajsa boat gives the Grand Harbour view kids remember.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • I Pupi Pizzeria Valletta — excellent-value pizza, 4.9★, ~$12–20 pp
  • Casa Sotto — Roman pizza in Valletta, top family pizza pick
  • Sally Port Pizzeria — pizza/pasta in Valletta, 4.8★; familiar dinner fallback
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Mdina ↗ — quieter medieval “Silent City” if Valletta feels too hot or crowded.
  • Ghajn Tuffieha ↗ — proper sandy beach day without Comino boat logistics.
  • Ramla Bay ↗ — red-sand beach version of a Gozo day if a full island loop is too much.

This is the water-temperature fix in its purest form. Book the new free Blue Lagoon access pass before you go.

Blue Lagoon + Comino

PhotosIG · Blue LagoonIG · CominoFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

The 2026 visitor system requires a free pre-booked Blue Lagoon pass to step on land, with morning/afternoon/evening slots; commercial boat trips commonly ~€30–45 adult / child discounts.

June weather

Air ~80–84°F / 69–71°F. Comino water should be around 73–74°F on 6/21/2027, excellent compared with Portugal and usually calmer/warmer-feeling inside the lagoon.

Save Book the free slot yourself before buying a tour; choose the morning slot for clearer water and fewer boats.
Splurge Private or semi-private Comino/Gozo boat, ~€250–450 family, avoids packed party boats.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • I Pupi Pizzeria Valletta — excellent-value pizza, 4.9★, ~$12–20 pp
  • Casa Sotto — Roman pizza in Valletta, top family pizza pick
  • Sally Port Pizzeria — pizza/pasta in Valletta, 4.8★; familiar dinner fallback
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Għajn Tuffieħa ↗ — Malta beach day with sand and no Comino boat logistics.
  • Ghajn Tuffieha ↗ — proper sandy beach day without Comino boat logistics.
  • Ramla Bay ↗ — red-sand beach version of a Gozo day if a full island loop is too much.

Gozo day trip

PhotosIG · GozoFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Gozo Channel return foot passenger €4.65 adult; children 3–12 €1.15; pay on return from Gozo. Fast ferry from Valletta is more, about €8.50 one way.

June weather

Air ~80–84°F / 69–71°F. Xlendi/Ramla water should be around 73–75°F on 6/22/2027.

Save Go as foot passengers and use a Gozo taxi/Bolt for key stops; taking a Malta rental car over adds cost and parking friction.
Splurge Private Gozo driver for 5–6 hrs, ~€180–280, lets you hit Victoria, Ramla, salt pans and Xlendi without bus waits.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Country Terrace — views near Mgarr; pasta, pizza-ish kids choices, ~$18–30 pp
  • Maldonado Bistro — Victoria bistro with pasta/burgers, ~$15–25 pp
  • Front Bar Xlendi — casual seafront, burgers/fries/sandwiches
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Ramla Bay ↗ — make it a red-sand beach day instead of a full island loop.
  • Three Cities waterfront ↗ — harbor views and old streets with a calmer feel than Valletta.
  • Popeye Village viewpoint ↗ — kitschy but kid-friendly photo stop near a scenic cove.

Cefalù beach day + La Rocca

Water-heavy, low-cost day

Est. ~$190 · La Rocca + beach set/food

SleepCefalù · night 2 of 3
PlanMorning La Rocca if cool, long beach block, old-town dinner
BalanceMostly swim

Make this the first full warm-water day: hike La Rocca while it is cool, then leave a long afternoon for the beach and an old-town dinner.

Cefalù old town + beach + La Rocca

PhotosIG · CefalùIG · La RoccaFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Beach/old town are free. La Rocca urban park is currently EUR5 full / EUR2.50 reduced ages 6-14; family estimate about EUR15.

Weather

Mid-June: warm coastal days, often upper 70s to low 80s F. Sea commonly runs about 72-75 F by mid/late June.

Save Make the best day nearly free: beach, old town, sunset harbor, grocery breakfast, and La Rocca only if the morning is cool.
Splurge Rent a beach set or do a short boat outing along the coast; confirm the chair/umbrella price before sitting.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Bottega Tivitti — pizzeria/burger seafront option; pizza, burgers, fries
  • Pasta e Pasti — casual pasta counter; tomato pasta and simple shapes work
  • 'Nna Principi — Italian/pizza spot with Margherita and penne pasta mentioned in current reviews
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Campofelice di Roccella ↩ — less postcard charm, but sometimes better-value beach lodging with parking.
  • Kalura Beach ↗ — clearer rocky cove near Cefalu when the town beach is crowded.
  • Monreale Cathedral ↗ — mosaic-heavy indoor/culture swap if Palermo street heat is too much.

Palermo gives Sicily its city texture, but the day stays flexible because kids may prefer Mondello after two hot hours in town.

Palermo historic core + Mondello

PhotosIG · PalermoIG · MondelloFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Street wandering is free. Palazzo dei Normanni / Palatine Chapel official current adult tickets vary by day/access; plan roughly EUR15.50-EUR19.50 adult and check the exact calendar.

Weather

June: Palermo is hotter and less breezy than Cefalù. Do culture early, then use Mondello for afternoon water if the city gets heavy.

Save Train from Cefalù into Palermo avoids city parking; Palermo-Cefalù rail is frequent and cheap enough for a family city day.
Splurge Palatine Chapel/Norman Palace is the paid anchor if mosaics matter; skip it if the kids need beach more than gold ceilings.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Bottega Tivitti — pizzeria/burger seafront option; pizza, burgers, fries
  • Pasta e Pasti — casual pasta counter; tomato pasta and simple shapes work
  • 'Nna Principi — Italian/pizza spot with Margherita and penne pasta mentioned in current reviews
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Monreale Cathedral ↩ — another mosaic masterpiece if Palermo feels too intense.
  • Mondello Beach ↗ — classic Palermo swim day when the kids need sand over churches.
  • Campofelice di Roccella ↗ — less charming than Cefalu, but easier parking and often better-value beach lodging.

Mon - 6/14/2027

Etna morning, coast afternoon

Nature + recovery swim

Est. ~$170-$420 · depends on cable car

SleepTaormina coast · night 2 of 3
Plan AFree Silvestri craters + picnic
Plan BCable car only if weather is clear

Etna is the nature pillar, not an automatic expensive summit day. Check visibility and volcanic conditions before buying anything above the free crater walks.

Mount Etna south slope

AllTrails
PhotosIG · Mount EtnaFlickrAllTrails
1 / 3
Cost

Free Silvestri craters are the budget plan. Cable-car-only prices have been reported around EUR50 adult / EUR30 kids 5-10; 4x4/guided higher-slope options can push a family toward EUR250-EUR320.

Weather

Mountain day: cooler and windier than the coast. Visibility drives value; do not buy expensive summit options if clouds/ash are poor.

Save Walk Silvestri craters for free, then spend the money on a beach dinner or boat day.
Splurge Cable car or 4x4 only on a clear morning; the 13-year-old will get more out of it than the 8-year-old if the weather cooperates.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Bam Bar — granita/brioche stop; easy snack win near Taormina old town
  • Da Cristina — arancini, pizza/focaccia-style quick food, fries-like comfort sides nearby
  • La Capinera / beach alternatives — Giardini-Naxos has broad pizza, pasta, burger, and beach-lunch options if Taormina menus are too adult
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Alcantara Gorge ↩ — cool-water nature day if Etna weather is bad or crater access is restricted.
  • Letojanni Beach ↗ — lower-key beach town north of Taormina when Isola Bella is packed.
  • Giardini-Naxos beach ↗ — more space, easier food, and simpler parking than tiny Isola Bella.

Taormina + Isola Bella

PhotosIG · TaorminaIG · Isola BellaFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Ancient Theatre official current fare: EUR14 full / EUR7 reduced. Isola Bella beach is free; beach sets/boat extras vary.

Weather

Mid/late June: warm to hot, with Ionian water around 72-75 F.

Save Stay below Taormina and use cable car/taxi into town; avoid old-town parking and lodging premiums.
Splurge Greek Theatre at golden hour plus an Isola Bella boat/swim stop; budget $120-250 family depending tour style.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Bam Bar — granita/brioche stop; easy snack win near Taormina old town
  • Da Cristina — arancini, pizza/focaccia-style quick food, fries-like comfort sides nearby
  • La Capinera / beach alternatives — Giardini-Naxos has broad pizza, pasta, burger, and beach-lunch options if Taormina menus are too adult
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Giardini-Naxos beach ↩ — easier beach logistics and lower prices if Taormina is crowded.
  • Alcantara Gorge ↗ — cool basalt canyon and water play when Etna weather turns cloudy or windy.
  • Castelmola ↗ — hilltop village above Taormina for views without committing to a beach afternoon.
