TL;DR · Excluded · 30/50 for reference

What you’d experience: Mostar's bridge and Kravica falls, the Bay of Kotor with Perast, Albania's Ksamil beaches and the Blue Eye spring, ending on Corfu.

Why it ranks here: The biggest adventure arc and warm 76–79°F swimming (Swim 5, Variety 5, Novelty 4 — only the Dubrovnik gateway repeats). Sunk by logistics and money: four countries with cross-border car rules (Ease 1), July peak pricing, and the board's worst ceiling at $10.6k–16.8k (Budget 1).

The Plan at a Glance

Mostar to Corfu arc

I kept the recommended four-base spine because it is the cleanest family version: water-heavy, low backtracking, and easy to end at Corfu airport. The original month route would be excellent with more time, but too many bases for this trip.

Route

PIT -> Dubrovnik -> Mostar -> Kotor -> Ksamil -> Corfu -> PIT

Dubrovnik is the preferred arrival gateway because Tirana makes the first day a 6.5-7 hour northbound drive before the route turns south again.

What got cut

Sarajevo, Durmitor, Tirana, Skopje, Matka, Ohrid

Those are the month-long extensions: Sarajevo adds a northern start, Durmitor is an inland detour, and the eastern loop swings far inland before doubling back toward Corfu.

Lodging split

3 + 3 + 3 + 3 nights

Three nights each in Mostar, Kotor, Ksamil, and Corfu keeps transfers tolerable while trimming the expensive extra island night.

Trip feel

~45% water · 30% towns · 25% nature

The daily plan intentionally stacks waterfalls, bay swims, beaches, boat time, and the Blue Eye around compact old-town exploring.

Why This Trip

The biggest adventure arc

Pick the Balkans if you want the most varied story: Mostar, Kravica, Kotor Bay, Ksamil, and Corfu in one southbound road trip with the warmest Europe-style swimming.

Choose this if

  • Swim 5 and Variety 5 are the case: the route stacks waterfalls, bay swims, Ksamil beaches, the Blue Eye, and Corfu boat time.
  • The 12-night shape uses 8 PTO days and moves mostly one direction from Dubrovnik to Corfu instead of looping back.
  • For kids, every region has an obvious payoff: bridges and falls in Mostar, boats and walls in Kotor, warm beaches in Ksamil, and Corfu to finish.

Reasons to pause

  • This is excluded for a reason: scorecard 30/50, Ease 1, Budget 1, and a $10.6k-$16.8k ceiling that can exceed the preferred maximum.
  • Four countries mean border crossings, cross-border rental-car rules, ferry timing, and more ways for one delay to cascade.
  • July peak pricing and heat are worse than the June finalists; choose this only if adventure outranks simplicity.

Where We Are Staying

Four bases, one direction

Each base earns its nights: Mostar for river-town texture, Kotor for the bay, Ksamil for clear water, and Corfu for the island finish.

3 nights · 7/2/2027–7/4/2027

Mostar, Bosnia and Herzegovina

Arrival base from Dubrovnik

Mostar keeps the first drive short after an overnight flight and gives the kids a compact old town, bridge views, cold river water, Blagaj, and Kravica without changing hotels.

Book: Family apartment or small hotel inside a 10-15 minute walk of Stari Most; current family-capable signals suggest roughly $90-$150/night in the July peak window.

3 nights · 7/5/2027–7/7/2027

Bay of Kotor, Montenegro

Bay swims, old towns, fortress views

Base in Kotor, Dobrota, or Prcanj for easy evenings on the water. The itinerary uses one Kotor day and one Perast/Budva/Sveti Stefan day.

Book: Apartment with parking outside the old-town walls; July family-capable bands are roughly $175-$300/night before taxes.

3 nights · 7/8/2027–7/10/2027

Ksamil or Saranda, Albania

Beach-heavy middle-south base

Ksamil is the clearest-water choice; Saranda is easier for restaurants, parking, and the ferry. Staying just outside central Ksamil can save money and stress.

Book: Apartment or family room with parking; July family rooms run roughly EUR140-EUR280/night in Ksamil, often less in Saranda.

3 nights · 7/11/2027–7/13/2027

Corfu, Greece

Island landing before flying home

The car logistics reset here: arrive as foot passengers, rent locally only if needed, and slow down with beaches, a boat day, and one compact Old Town evening before flying home.

Book: Family rental near Corfu Town, Gouvia, Dassia, or Paleokastritsa depending whether airport access or beach access matters more; July comparable rentals cluster around $250-$420/night.

Mostar Old Town + Stari Most

PhotosIG · MostarIG · Stari MostFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Old Bridge and bazaar are free. Koski Mehmed Pasha Mosque/minaret is paid; recent reports conflict around EUR7-EUR15, so ask at the door.

Weather

July: hot, often upper 80s to low 90s F daytime / upper 60s F nights. Neretva swimming is cold and local-condition dependent.

Save Stay walkable and skip paid viewpoints; the bridge, bazaar, riverbanks, and sunset angles are free.
Splurge Pay for the mosque/minaret viewpoint if open; budget roughly EUR30-EUR60 family depending the current tariff.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Restaurant-Pizzeria Megi — pizza, pasta, lasagna, steaks, cakes; plain pizza/pasta works
  • Nide Veze — pizza, sandwiches, pancakes, waffles; Margherita pizza is the easy order
  • Restoran Vodopad Kravice — at the falls; burger/fries, chicken, and pizza-style options reported
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Pocitelj ↩ — a compact Ottoman stone village if Mostar feels crowded.
  • Koski Mehmed Pasha Mosque viewpoint ↗ — quick terrace/minaret view over Stari Most if you want the bridge payoff without another long walk.
  • Blagaj Buna canals ↗ — riverside restaurants and ducks by the spring if the Tekke interior is not a priority.

Kravica Waterfalls

PhotosIG · Kravica WaterfallsFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Official 2026 pricing: 20 KM adult, 10 KM ages 7-18, under 7 free; parking 3 KM/hour or 6 KM/day. Family estimate: 60 KM plus parking.

Weather

Water is cool, roughly high 60s F to low 70s F in summer, but swimmable in hot weather.

Save Bring towels, water shoes, and snacks so the falls are a half-day swim/picnic instead of a restaurant day.
Splurge Use the on-site restaurant and stay through late afternoon light; budget about 80-120 KM for an easy meal.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Nide Veze — pizza, sandwiches, pancakes, waffles; Margherita pizza is the easy order

  • Buna canals near Blagaj ↩ — less of a swim spectacle, but easier if Kravica crowds look rough.
  • Mogorjelo Roman villa ↗ — small ruins stop near Capljina when culture wins over waterfall crowds.
  • Kravica picnic edge ↗ — quieter riverside pause if the main waterfall viewing area is packed.

Kotor Old Town + City Walls

PhotosIG · KotorFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

City walls/San Giovanni fortress currently reported at EUR15 per person, under 12 free; family estimate about EUR45.

Weather

July: around low-to-mid 80s F days / upper 60s to low 70s nights. Bay water averages about 77-79 F.

Save Climb early, carry water, and skip paid boat tours on this day; old-town wandering is free.

Travel Tips

The stuff nobody tells you

Updated July 2026 for the July 2027 travel window; re-check flight, rental, and ferry rules before paying because those are the unstable pieces.

Book in roughly this order

  1. Open-jaw flights · first, because route choice depends on DBV/TIA pricing
  2. Cross-border rental quote · before lodging is locked
  3. Lodging with parking · immediately after car plan
  4. Saranda-Corfu ferry · once car drop timing is known
  5. Corfu local transport · after lodging location
  6. Boat days · 1-2 weeks out, then weather-check

  • Documents

    Avoid border surprises

    • Passports. Required for every border and the Saranda-Corfu ferry.
    • IDP. Bring one even if some agencies do not ask; it is cheap insurance.
    • Rental paperwork. Carry written country permissions and green-card/insurance proof.

  • Car Strategy

    The make-or-break logistics

    • Get it in writing. Croatia, Bosnia, Montenegro, Albania, one-way drop, and ferry prohibition should all be explicit.
    • Photograph the car. Do a full video at pickup and drop-off, including wheels and underside edges.
    • Do not ferry the car. Plan the Balkan car to end before Corfu.

  • Money

    Small cash still matters

    • Currencies. EUR for Montenegro/Greece/ferry, KM for Bosnia, ALL for Albania.
    • Cards. Good at hotels and many restaurants, less reliable for parking, small tickets, beach sets.
    • ATMs. Use bank ATMs and decline dynamic currency conversion.

  • Heat + Swim

    Make the day survivable

    • Early starts. Fortress, Butrint, and Porto Timoni are morning activities.
    • Water shoes. Useful for Kravica, bay ladders, Ksamil rocks, and Corfu coves.
    • Shade tax. Lounger sets can be worth it when the alternative is four overheated people.

  • Food

    Picky-kid fail-safes

    • Pizza/pasta anchors. Every base has at least one researched plain-food option.
    • Road snacks. The Kotor-Ksamil day needs grocery snacks before departure.
    • Late dinners. Balkan/Greek summer dinners run later; feed kids something plain before scenic dinners if needed.

  • Ferry + Time

    The Corfu crossing has one hidden trap

    • Time zone. Greece is one hour ahead of Albania in summer.
    • Passport control. Arrive early and do not stack a tight airport plan after the ferry.
    • Tickets. Book online when possible, but keep cash/card flexibility at Saranda port.
