Arrive Funchal, cable car, old town evening

Soft landing

Est. $180-$320 - car pickup, cable car, simple dinner

BaseFunchal - night 1 of 4
Easy winCable car up, toboggan down

Keep arrival deliberately easy: pick up the car, check in, then use the Monte cable car and toboggan as the jet-lag-proof kid hook. Confirm the operating day, eat early in the Zona Velha, and save the alpine start for a rested morning.

Funchal old town + Monte cable car + toboggans

PhotosIG · FunchalIG · MonteFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Cable car EUR 22 RT / EUR 16 one-way adult, ages 7-14 half price - one-way up for the family is ~EUR 48. Monte toboggan (2026 prices): EUR 40 per 2-person sled, cash only, closed Sundays - two sleds = EUR 80. Monte Palace garden EUR 18 adult, kids to 14 free.

Weather

June Funchal: 72-75 F days / 61-63 F nights, ~10 hours of sun and only ~5 rain days - one of the driest months on the island.

Save Ride the cable car one-way up (EUR 48, not EUR 76 RT), toboggan partway down, and finish by Bolt/taxi - the classic route that skips paying for the ride down twice.
Splurge The toboggan itself IS the splurge at EUR 80 for two wicker sleds - a 130-year-old tradition and the single most kid-memorable EUR 80 on the island.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Pizzaria Xaramba — old-town pizzeria by the fort; plain margherita is the safe order
  • Hamburgueria do Mercado — burgers and fries by the market; a plain burger runs about EUR 10
  • Il Basilico — Lido promenade Italian; pizza, plain pasta, and highchairs
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Mercado dos Lavradores + old-town painted doors ↩ — the free version of a Funchal afternoon: market fruit tasting upstairs, painted-door street art in the Zona Velha, gelato by the fort.
  • Jardim Botanico da Madeira ↗ — terraced gardens and city views; a good fog or heat substitute for higher viewpoints.
  • Lido pools ↗ — easy managed swim with showers and food if open-water conditions are not friendly.

Pico do Arieiro: Stairway to Heaven ridge (PR1)

AllTrails
PhotosIG · Pico do ArieiroFlickrAllTrails
1 / 3
Cost

PR1 permit EUR 10.50 pp booked on SIMplifica (EUR 7 with a licensed operator); kids 12 and under free, so the family pays ~EUR 31.50. Summit parking free but full before sunrise on clear days. Shared sunrise transfer alternative: EUR 30-40 pp.

Weather

Pre-dawn summit (1,818 m): 41-50 F even in June - real jackets and hats. The sea-of-clouds inversion is a morning phenomenon; clouds typically swallow the peaks by afternoon. June is one of Madeira's driest months.

Save Self-drive at 5:15 am instead of booking transfer seats: the family saves roughly $120-$160 and keeps the schedule flexible for the cloud lottery.
Splurge Book the guided sunrise transfer (EUR 30-40 pp) if nobody wants to drive mountain hairpins in the dark - pickup ~5:00-5:30 am for a ~6:50 sunrise.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Pizzaria Xaramba — old-town pizzeria by the fort; plain margherita is the safe order
  • Hamburgueria do Mercado — burgers and fries by the market; a plain burger runs about EUR 10
  • Il Basilico — Lido promenade Italian; pizza, plain pasta, and highchairs
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Pico Ruivo from Achada do Teixeira (PR1.2) ↩ — the gentler back route to Madeira's highest summit - a real substitute if the PR1 first section is closed or the exposure feels like too much for the 8-year-old.
  • Risco waterfall only ↗ — flatter Rabacal branch with a tall waterfall when 25 Fontes feels like too much walking.
  • Queimadas Forest Park ↗ — thatched houses, picnic lawns, and easy laurel-forest paths without a long levada commitment.

25 Fontes levada, then Camara de Lobos golden hour

Waterfall + village day

Est. $120-$240 - permits, shuttle, skywalk, dinner

MorningRabacal / 25 Fontes (PR6)
Late dayCamara de Lobos + Cabo Girao skywalk

Levada walking is the flat, kid-friendly lower-exposure counterpoint to the alpine ridge day: water channels, tunnels of green, and a waterfall pool payoff. Bail to the short Risco branch if the 8-year-old is done. End with fishing boats and the 580 m skywalk on the way home.

25 Fontes + Risco levada (PR6, Rabacal)

AllTrails
PhotosIG · 25 FontesIG · RiscoFlickrAllTrails
1 / 3
Cost

Permit EUR 4.50 pp (kids 12 and under free) = ~EUR 13.50 family, booked on SIMplifica. Rabacal parking free; the access-road shuttle is EUR 5 one-way / EUR 8 return, cash only.

Weather

June: the Rabacal plateau sits in the cloud belt - expect cooler, mistier air than Funchal and pack a layer. Trails are mostly flat levada walking with ~300 m of stairs near the falls.

Save Start before the ~10-11 am shuttle and walk the 2 km access road down (~40 min) - saves EUR 32 family and beats the crowds to the falls.
Splurge None needed - this day is nearly free. If energy is high, add the 30-minute Risco waterfall branch (PR6.1) at no extra cost.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Anonimo — some of the island's best Neapolitan pizza; margherita for the kids
  • Pizza Cafe (Camara de Lobos) — generous pizza and pasta portions steps from the harbor
  • Restaurante Santo Antonio — espetada house since 1966; grilled beef/chicken skewers, fries, and bolo do caco bread
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Risco waterfall only (PR6.1) ↩ — the built-in bail-out: the Risco branch is a short, flat walk to a 100 m waterfall if the full 9-11 km round trip looks like too much for the 8-year-old.
  • Queimadas Forest Park ↗ — thatched houses, picnic lawns, and easy laurel-forest paths without a long levada commitment.
  • Ribeiro Frio and Balcoes ↗ — short, family-friendly forest walk with a big-view payoff if clouds cooperate.

Camara de Lobos + Cabo Girao Skywalk

PhotosIG · Camara de LobosIG · Cabo GiraoFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Camara de Lobos harbor is free. Cabo Girao skywalk is EUR 5 pp since Jan 2026, kids 12 and under free - plan EUR 15 worst case (whether the 13-year-old is exempt is ambiguous in current sources).

Weather

June: the sunny south coast - 72-75 F afternoons. Golden hour is the move: fishing boats glow and the 580 m cliff view softens after the tour buses leave.

Save Do this as the late-afternoon tail of the 25 Fontes day rather than a separate outing - it is 15 minutes from Funchal and needs no tickets beyond the skywalk.
Splurge An espetada dinner at Santo Antonio in Estreito - the skewers-and-fries house the locals drive to, and a legitimately picky-kid-safe splurge.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Anonimo — some of the island's best Neapolitan pizza; margherita for the kids
  • Pizza Cafe (Camara de Lobos) — generous pizza and pasta portions steps from the harbor
  • Restaurante Santo Antonio — espetada house since 1966; grilled beef/chicken skewers, fries, and bolo do caco bread
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Ponta do Sol sunset ↩ — the island's sunniest village, 15 minutes further west - a good swap if the family wants a slower waterfront evening instead of the skywalk.
  • Santa Catarina Park ↗ — low-effort green space above the harbor for a quick kid reset before dinner.
  • Mercado dos Lavradores ↗ — colorful market + fruit stop in the old town when you want a lighter Funchal hour.

Fanal fog forest + Porto Moniz lava pools

Otherworldly north coast

Est. $100-$220 - pools, lunch, fuel

MorningFanal - go only if there IS fog
AfternoonPorto Moniz pools, EUR 12 family
Backup roleArieiro retry slot

This day has a double job: it is the ancient-forest-and-lava-pools loop, and it is the Arieiro backup slot. If Thursday's summit was clouded out, do the sunrise retry first and compress this loop into the afternoon. Fanal without fog is skippable; Porto Moniz is not.

Fanal fog forest (Paul da Serra)

PhotosIG · FanalFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Currently free, with a large free lot at the Fanal forestry post. Since Jan 2026 access is designated-paths-only with a daily visitor cap; a paid-entry model is under study - verify closer to travel.

Weather

June: Fanal sits at ~1,150 m in the cloud belt - fog is most likely in the morning and on humid days, and June afternoons often burn clear. The fog IS the show; a clear Fanal is just a pasture with old trees.

Save Fanal costs nothing - the save is sequencing. Check the webcams/weather that morning and only commit when fog is actually sitting on the plateau.
Splurge Nothing to buy here. Spend the saved money on lunch at the Porto Moniz pools 25 minutes downhill.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Restaurante Cachalote — built on the lava rocks beside the pools; burgers and simple grills, the location does the work
  • Restaurante Orca — pool-view terrace; burgers and simple mains
  • Restaurante Santo Antonio — espetada-and-fries dinner stop on the drive back south via Encumeada
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Sao Vicente valley drive ↩ — if the plateau is stubbornly clear, drop through the dramatic north-coast valley road instead and get to the pools early.
  • Pico Ruivo from Achada do Teixeira ↗ — shorter back-door summit route if the Arieiro ridge is closed, foggy, or too exposed.
  • Risco waterfall only ↗ — flatter Rabacal branch with a tall waterfall when 25 Fontes feels like too much walking.

Porto Moniz natural lava pools

PhotosIG · Porto MonizFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

EUR 3 pp for everyone age 3+ = EUR 12 family, open 9 am-7 pm in summer, with changing rooms, lockers, showers, lifeguards, and a snack bar. The rougher Cachalote pools on the west side are free.

Weather

June sea: ~70 F - brisk but swimmable, and the lava pools are calm even when the open Atlantic is rough. This is Madeira's one real swim stop.

Save The paid pools are already the cheap option at EUR 12 for the family - bring towels and swim shoes and skip renting anything.
Splurge Lunch on the rocks at Cachalote or Orca with the surf exploding behind the glass - pay for the view once.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Restaurante Cachalote — built on the lava rocks beside the pools; burgers and simple grills, the location does the work
  • Restaurante Orca — pool-view terrace; burgers and simple mains
  • Restaurante Santo Antonio — espetada-and-fries dinner stop on the drive back south via Encumeada
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Seixal natural pools + black-sand beach ↩ — smaller, wilder pools and a black-sand beach 15 minutes east - a quieter swap if Porto Moniz is packed.
  • Praia da Calheta ↗ — sheltered golden-sand beach; the easiest true sand-and-swim fallback on Madeira.
  • Faja dos Padres ↗ — cable-car descent to a quiet beach/vineyard terrace when the family wants a half-day adventure.

Blue Lagoon + Comino

PhotosIG · Blue LagoonIG · CominoFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

The 2026 visitor system requires a free pre-booked Blue Lagoon pass to step on land, with morning/afternoon/evening slots; commercial boat trips commonly ~€30–45 adult / child discounts.

June weather

Air ~80–84°F / 69–71°F. Comino water should be around 73–74°F on 6/21/2027, excellent compared with Portugal and usually calmer/warmer-feeling inside the lagoon.

Save Book the free slot yourself before buying a tour; choose the morning slot for clearer water and fewer boats.
Splurge Private or semi-private Comino/Gozo boat, ~€250–450 family, avoids packed party boats.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • I Pupi Pizzeria Valletta — excellent-value pizza, 4.9★, ~$12–20 pp
  • Casa Sotto — Roman pizza in Valletta, top family pizza pick
  • Sally Port Pizzeria — pizza/pasta in Valletta, 4.8★; familiar dinner fallback
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Għajn Tuffieħa ↗ — Malta beach day with sand and no Comino boat logistics.
  • Ghajn Tuffieha ↗ — proper sandy beach day without Comino boat logistics.
  • Ramla Bay ↗ — red-sand beach version of a Gozo day if a full island loop is too much.

Gozo day trip

PhotosIG · GozoFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Gozo Channel return foot passenger €4.65 adult; children 3–12 €1.15; pay on return from Gozo. Fast ferry from Valletta is more, about €8.50 one way.

June weather

Air ~80–84°F / 69–71°F. Xlendi/Ramla water should be around 73–75°F on 6/22/2027.

Save Go as foot passengers and use a Gozo taxi/Bolt for key stops; taking a Malta rental car over adds cost and parking friction.
Splurge Private Gozo driver for 5–6 hrs, ~€180–280, lets you hit Victoria, Ramla, salt pans and Xlendi without bus waits.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Country Terrace — views near Mgarr; pasta, pizza-ish kids choices, ~$18–30 pp
  • Maldonado Bistro — Victoria bistro with pasta/burgers, ~$15–25 pp
  • Front Bar Xlendi — casual seafront, burgers/fries/sandwiches
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Ramla Bay ↗ — make it a red-sand beach day instead of a full island loop.
  • Three Cities waterfront ↗ — harbor views and old streets with a calmer feel than Valletta.
  • Popeye Village viewpoint ↗ — kitschy but kid-friendly photo stop near a scenic cove.
