Piazza San Marco at kid pace

Towns & squares

Est. $220 · Campanile, meals

SleepVenice · night 2 of 2
MorningSan Marco early, before the heat
OptionalCampanile lift for the lagoon view

Do the great square early while it is cool and half-empty. The free piazza and facade are the memory; the Campanile elevator is the one paid add-on worth it. Keep the afternoon loose.

Piazza San Marco & St. Mark's Basilica

PhotosIG · sanmarcoIG · veniceIG · basilicaFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Piazza free. Basilica nave €10/person (pre-book). Campanile lift €15/person (~€60/family).

Weather

82°F day / 68°F night — humid. The square bakes at midday; go early or at dusk.

Save Skip the paid Basilica interior — the free piazza and facade are the photo. Saves €40–100 and keeps the day light, per the no-death-march plan.
Splurge Pre-book the Campanile lift (€60/family) for the 323-ft lagoon view — the elevator is far kinder on kid legs than the stairs.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • 1000 Gourmet Venezia — Neapolitan pizza near the piazza, reliably packed with families
  • Aciugheta — Venetian trattoria near San Zaccaria, wood-fired kids’ pizza plus cicchetti
  • 6342 A Le Tole — 30+ pizzas and housemade pasta in Castello
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Doge’s Palace courtyard — free-adjacent and a better backup if the Basilica line feels like too much with kids.
  • Bridge of Sighs — a two-minute photo stop just behind the palace.
  • Gelato on the Riva — a waterfront cone with the lagoon view instead of a second church.

Lago di Braies — the emerald warm-up

Alpine lake

Est. $150 · shuttle/boat, rifugio lunch

SleepSan Vito di Cadore (Cortina) · night 2 of 5
EarlyBeat the 9am valley car-closure
WalkFree lakeshore loop ~35 min

Ease into the Dolomites with the gentlest, most famous lake. Go early — the valley closes to private cars at 9am from 7/1/2027 (reserve at prags.bz), and morning is when the water is a mirror.

Lago di Braies

PhotosIG · lagodibraiesIG · dolomitesIG · pragserwildseeFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Parking €7 (P2) to €12 (P3, lakeside). Rowboat: shared €20/person, private 45-min €55. Valley car-closed 9am–4pm from 7/1/2027 — reserve at prags.bz.

Altitude

60°F day / 42°F night at lake level (1,496m) — the cold lake keeps mornings crisp; go before 9am for glass-still reflections.

Save Skip the boat and walk the free ~35-minute lakeshore loop — same emerald water, same boathouse, zero queue. Saves ~€55–80 for the family.
Splurge A private 45-minute rowboat (€55) at golden hour for the postcard frame — wooden hull, the boathouse behind you, the peaks doubled in the water.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Panorama (Hotel Lago di Braies) — lake-view terrace, South Tyrolean; simple pasta for kids, reserve ahead
  • Chalet snack counter — fries, sandwiches, simple pasta, the fast picky-eater lunch
  • Ristorante Lago di Braies — independent lakeside spot, confirm pizza on the day’s menu
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Lago di Carezza — a zero-hike, drive-up alpine lake below the Latemar with no reservation system.
  • Lago di Dobbiaco — a flat, stroller-easy lake loop close to the base.
  • Prato Piazza (Plätzwiese) — a high meadow plateau if the family wants views without a climb.

Wed - 6/9/2027

Tre Cime di Lavaredo — the big one

Epic hike

Est. $180 · toll road, trail lunch

SleepSan Vito di Cadore (Cortina) · night 3 of 5
SlotPre-booked toll road, 7–9am
Loop~6mi, cable-car-free but a gentle grade

The signature Dolomites day. The toll road drives you to 2,333m so the loop starts high — the 8-year-old walks under the three peaks without a summit push, the 13-year-old gets a real trail. Start early to beat the storms — and from the same Rifugio Auronzo car park you can bolt on the short Cadini di Misurina viewpoint at sunrise (see below).

Tre Cime di Lavaredo

PhotosIG · trecimeIG · dreizinnenIG · dolomitesFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Toll road to Rifugio Auronzo €40/car (pre-book a timed slot at auronzo.info). Rifugio lunch ~€60–80/family.

Altitude

62°F day / 38°F night at 2,333m — afternoon storms are common; start the loop by 9am and be down by mid-afternoon.

Save Book the 7–9am toll slot for the best light and thinnest crowd, and pack sandwiches for the Locatelli viewpoint instead of a rifugio lunch (€15–18/plate × 4).
Splurge Sleep at Rifugio Locatelli (~€60–70/person half-board) to get the blue-hour and sunrise faces without the day-trippers — bring cash, the huts don’t take cards.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Rifugio Auronzo — self-service at the trailhead: pasta, wurstel, fries, polenta; easiest kid meal on the mountain
  • Rifugio Locatelli — the classic Three-Peaks view hut; plain polenta for picky eaters
  • Rifugio Lavaredo — trailside on the loop, same Alpine self-service and a good fallback if Locatelli is packed
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Lago di Sorapis — a harder, quieter hike to a milky-turquoise lake (fixed cables) for the 13-year-old.
  • Monte Piana — a WWI open-air museum plateau above Lago di Misurina with trenches, tunnels, and 360° views.
  • Lago di Misurina lakeshore — the easy, flat option at the foot of the toll road if legs are done.

Cadini di Misurina Viewpoint

PhotosIG · cadinidimisurinaIG · dolomitesIG · misurinaFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

No extra toll — same Rifugio Auronzo car park as Tre Cime (€40/car, one timed slot covers both). The viewpoint hike itself is free: ~3.9km out-and-back, ~1hr, only 144m of gain on path #117.

Trail

Easy hike, one exposed catch. Short and mostly gentle from the car park — but the famous photo promontory is a narrow ridge with cliff drop-offs. Doable with kids if you keep the 8-year-old close and stop short of the knife-edge tip; the 13-year-old is fine with care in dry weather. A right-side contour path skips the exposed crest entirely. Skip it if wet, icy, or windy.

Save This is the rare free epic — no lift, no separate ticket. Do it first, at sunrise, before the Tre Cime loop while the light rakes the needles and the ridge is empty, then walk back to the car and start the big loop.
Splurge Book a local sunrise photo guide from Misurina — they know exactly where the family can stand safely for the postcard frame and handle the pre-dawn timing so you don't.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Rifugio Auronzo — right at the trailhead; self-service pasta, wurstel, fries before or after the walk
  • Rifugio Col de Varda — the hut path #117 heads toward; chairlift-served, easy add for a strudel stop
  • Lakeside at Lago di Misurina — pizza and gelato by the lake at the foot of the toll road
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Right-side contour path — the same viewpoint area without going over the exposed crest, if heights are a concern.
  • Lago di Misurina loop — a flat, stroller-easy lakeshore walk with a playground if the family skips the ridge.
  • Monte Piana — a WWI trench-and-tunnel plateau above the lake for a history-flavored alternative.

Seceda’s ridge by cable car

Cable-car alpine

Est. $300 · cableway (family), lunch

SleepSan Vito di Cadore (Cortina) · night 4 of 5
Cable carOrtisei -> Seceda, timed slot
WalkRidge stroll, or hike down via Rifugio Firenze

The cable car does the climbing so everyone stands on the 2,500m Seceda ridge — the tilted-earth view of the Odle spires. Do the ridge walk before noon; willing hikers can ride up one-way and walk down to turn the ticket into the day’s trek.

Seceda & Alpe di Siusi

PhotosIG · secedaIG · alpedisiusiIG · valgardenaFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Ortisei–Seceda cableway round trip ~€74 adult / €37 junior (8–15), under-8 free — ~€222/family. Timed-slot pre-booking required.

Altitude

57°F day / 38°F night on the 2,500m ridge — very exposed and windy; do the ridge walk before noon, before the storms build.

Save Buy one-way UP (€49 adult/€25 junior) and hike down via Rifugio Firenze — it turns the priciest ticket of the leg into the day’s epic hike and saves ~€25/adult.
Splurge The full Seceda–Rifugio Firenze–Baita Troier circuit with a mid-ridge rifugio lunch — a half-day alpine trek under the Odle spires with three hut stops.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Baita Sanon — Alpe di Siusi, family-run Tyrolean/Ladin, explicitly kid-friendly, plain plates on request
  • Rifugio Firenze — on the ridge circuit; pasta, polenta, dumplings and an Odle-peaks terrace
  • Ristorante Bullaccia (Puflatsch) — Alpe di Siusi, fast-serving South Tyrolean for small kids
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Passo Giau — drive up the switchbacks to a wide-open meadow saddle with 360° views; no cable car, no timed slot.
  • Alpe di Siusi (Compatsch) — Europe’s largest alpine meadow, gentle and stroller-friendly by its own gondola.
  • Ortisei town — a pretty Val Gardena stroll if the ridge is socked in.

Cinque Torri & Cortina — the lighter day

Easy hike + town

Est. $200 · chairlift, cable car, dinner

SleepSan Vito di Cadore (Cortina) · night 5 of 5
MorningCinque Torri chairlift + WWI loop
AfternoonCortina town / Faloria cable car

Use the short Cinque Torri loop for a lighter alpine finale: WWI trenches beneath wild towers, then Cortina for a stroll and gelato.

Cinque Torri

PhotosIG · cinquetorriIG · dolomitesIG · cortinaFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Chairlift return €27.50 adult (one-way €20.50); under-8 free, minors discounted — budget ~€75–85/family. 5% card discount in 2026.

Altitude

60°F day / 40°F night at Rifugio Scoiattoli (2,225m) — short and easy, so it forgives a late-morning start after a slow breakfast.

Save Pay by credit card at the base for the built-in 5% discount, then walk the free ~2km WWI-trench loop once you’re up — one of the cheapest “epic scenery” stops of the whole trip.
Splurge Add the beginner via-ferrata-lite from Rifugio Scoiattoli toward Averau (short, guided) for the 13-year-old — a real climbing memory while the rest of the family relaxes on the terrace.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Rifugio Scoiattoli — right under the towers; casunziei ravioli and polenta, flag ahead for a plain-pasta kid plate
  • Rifugio Averau — a 30-min walk on, sommelier-run homemade pasta; confirm a simple option
  • Rifugio Cinque Torri — casual at the base of the towers; casunziei, ricotta strudel, homemade bread
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Rifugio Lagazuoi — a 5-minute cable car to a WWI tunnel network and a panoramic hut at Passo Falzarego.
  • Nuvolau / Averau loop — a longer scramble to a 360° summit view for the strong-legged.
  • Lago Ghedina — a quiet, forest-ringed little lake near Cortina for an easy afternoon.

Cortina d'Ampezzo & the Faloria Cable Car

PhotosIG · cortinaIG · dolomitesIG · faloriaFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Faloria cable car ~€27 adult / ~€20 under-16 (~€94/family). Freccia nel Cielo to Cima Tofana €45 adult round trip.

Weather

65°F day / 45°F night in town (1,224m) — mornings are the reliably clear window; storms roll through most afternoons.

Save Ride Faloria (~half the price of Freccia nel Cielo) for a comparable panorama, and window-shop Corso Italia for free — the 2026-Olympics town is a stroll, not a ticket.
Splurge Freccia nel Cielo all the way to Cima Tofana (3,244m, €45 adult) — the single highest, most dramatic viewpoint reachable without technical climbing on the whole trip.

Family & picky-kid eats

Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Lago di Sorapis viewpoint (Passo Tre Croci) — roadside massif views instead of a second cable car if legs are tired.
  • Rifugio Lagazuoi + WWI tunnels — a cable-car-and-history half day at Passo Falzarego.
  • Mercato / gelato on Corso Italia — a low-key town afternoon before the flight south.
