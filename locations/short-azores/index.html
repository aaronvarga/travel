Entry & Documents

Passports and essentials

Documents, driving, money, health, and connectivity for this trip. Time-sensitive items (especially ETIAS) can change — re-check the linked official sources close to departure.

Portugal (incl. Madeira and the Azores)

PassportValid ≥3 months beyond your planned EU departure, and issued within the last 10 years.
ETIASScheduled to begin operations in the last quarter of 2026; likely mandatory for eligible visa-exempt visitors by June 2027, but recheck the official launch date. — ~$22 per eligible traveler (set as €20; subject to change). Under-18s and over-70s are exempt.
VisaNone for US citizens — visa-free up to 90 days per 180.
Driving permitRecommended, not legally required. Get one from AAA before you go; some rental insurers ask for it.
Rental carsRental fleet is mostly manual — book an automatic early and expect a premium. Min age ~21; under-25 surcharge ~$7–17/day.
TollsElectronic tolls (Via Verde). Rentals usually include a transponder (~$1.50–2.50/day + tolls); confirm toll handling when booking.
MoneyCards widely accepted in hotels/restaurants/shops. Carry ~$55–110 in euros — small cafés and family spots may be cash/Multibanco only.
TippingNot expected; 5–10% appreciated for good service (cash).
Tap waterSafe in Lisbon and most cities. Algarve tap can taste earthy (hard water) — bottled if sensitive.
Emergency112 (EU-wide, free, mobile or landline).
ConnectivityHolafly or Airalo Eurolink work well; buy before departure.
ETIAS is a pre-travel authorization for visa-free visitors incl. US citizens. The official EU launch timing has shifted before, so reconfirm rules, fee, and eligibility close to departure.

Sources: US State Dept — Schengen passport rules · EU — ETIAS official · Via Verde visitors

Packing

What to bring

A family baseline that holds for every trip, plus items tuned to this route's conditions.

Family baseline

  • Passports / IDs + 2 photocopies stored separately (see the Entry & Documents section)
  • Debit + 2 credit cards (call issuers re: travel); some local cash on arrival
  • Phone chargers, a multi-port USB brick, and a power bank for long transit days
  • Refillable water bottles for the whole family
  • Kids' comfort items, tablets pre-loaded with downloads for flights/drives
  • Small first-aid kit: pain/fever meds (adult + kids), band-aids, motion-sickness tabs, any prescriptions in original bottles
  • Sunscreen (SPF 50), after-sun, insect repellent
  • Light day pack + a foldable tote for beach/market runs
  • Copies of bookings (flights, lodging, car, tickets) saved offline

Hiking days

  • Broken-in trail shoes / light hikers for everyone
  • Daypack with rain shell — mountain weather turns fast
  • Headlamp for tunnels, early starts or low-light trail sections
  • Trekking layers; blister plasters

Beach & water days

  • Swimsuits (2 each), quick-dry towels, rash guards for the kids
  • Water shoes for rocky entries, reefs or hot sand
  • Goggles + a mesh bag for wet gear
  • Dry bag for phones/keys on boat days

Rain / changeable weather

  • Packable rain jackets over umbrellas (wind)
  • A spare set of dry clothes in the day pack
  • Ziplocs to protect electronics

This trip specifically

  • Microclimate layers: light fleece and rain shell even when Ponta Delgada is warm.
  • Pool kit: water shoes and quick-dry towels for volcanic entries.
  • Road kit: phone mount, offline map and motion-sickness medicine.
  • Trail kit: grippy shoes, hats, headlamps and refillable bottles.

What June Feels Like

Warm coast, changeable peaks, refreshing water

São Miguel rewards families who dress in layers and treat the daily forecast as an invitation to choose the right side of the island.

Mild, mostly dry coast

Ponta Delgada is usually comfortable for walking and outdoor dinners in June. The peaks can still be cloudy, windy and much cooler.

Swimming is a bonus

Dona Beija and Terra Nostra offer managed water access, but the Atlantic remains refreshing and swell can change the plan.

Simple days from Ponta Delgada

Every outing returns to the same apartment, with no ferry, extra flight or hotel move. The only complicated choice is finding a good protected fare to the island.
