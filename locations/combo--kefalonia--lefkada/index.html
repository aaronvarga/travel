Myrtos cliff-road reveal, then Assos harbor

PhotosIG · madeiraIG · kefaloniaIG · ionianFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Myrtos viewpoints and Assos wandering are free; budget sunbeds/parking only if used. Myrtos’s steep shelf and changing swell make it a look/wade beach for the younger child, not the primary swim.

June planning conditions

June is warm and dry on Kefalonia: plan around roughly 78–84°F air and 72–74°F sea as historical proxies. Mornings are likelier to be calmer; west-facing exposure can build later.

Save Lead with the free cliff-road photo and have lunch in Assos rather than renting Myrtos sunbeds all day.
Splurge Assos harbor lunch at a waterside taverna after the viewpoint.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Platanos, Assos — pizza, pasta and grilled options.
  • Nefeli-Anait, Assos — simple taverna dishes and fries.
  • Sami groceries — pack water and beach food.
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Assos harbor swim if Myrtos surf is up.
  • Skip the beach descent if parking/heat makes the cliff view enough.

Mon - 6/14/2027

West-coast beaches

Porto Katsiki, Egremni & Kathisma

Est. $150 · parking, sunbeds, dinner

SleepLefkada (Nydri) · night 2 of 5
TimingGo early: west beaches chop up in the afternoon wind

Porto Katsiki & the west-coast beaches

PhotosIG · portokatsikiIG · egremniIG · lefkadaFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Lefkada’s west coast is a run of white-cliff, turquoise-water beaches. Porto Katsiki is the postcard — ~80–100 steps down from a paid clifftop lot (~€8–15/day, half-price after 4pm; no beach fee). Kathisma is the big, easy, organised beach (free lot, sunbeds, tavernas) — the family-friendly pick. Egremni is currently boat-only after a June 2025 municipal closure of the land route (re-verify for 2027); day boats reach it from Nydri/Vasiliki.

West-coast beaches

Go in the morning. These west-facing beaches are calm at dawn, but the NW Maistro builds from midday and brings real chop and bigger waves by afternoon — every source says visit early for calm water and a parking spot. Kathisma is the most sheltered and stroller-friendly; Porto Katsiki’s steep climb is hot at midday.

Save Do Kathisma (free parking, sunbeds, easy access) as the main swim and Porto Katsiki as a morning photo-and-dip; skip paid sunbeds and bring shade.
Splurge A boat trip from Vasiliki/Nydri that reaches Porto Katsiki and boat-only Egremni from the water, avoiding the cliff stairs entirely.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Rachi, Exanthia — famous sunset-view taverna above the west coast, simple options for kids
  • Kathisma beach bars — fries, toasties, and pasta right on the sand
  • Pack water + snacks — Porto Katsiki has only a small kantina
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Kathisma over Porto Katsiki if the kids won’t do the cliff stairs.
  • Kavalikefta for a quieter, wilder west-coast cove (narrow access road).
  • Boat to Egremni/Porto Katsiki for the cliff beaches without the climb.

Agios Nikitas + Vasiliki

Car-free village and a windsurf bay

Est. $170 · water taxi, lesson, dinner

SleepLefkada (Nydri) · night 3 of 5
MorningAgios Nikitas village + Milos beach (water taxi)
AfternoonOptional windsurf lesson at Vasiliki

A change of pace: the pretty car-free village of Agios Nikitas and a water-taxi hop to Milos beach in the calm morning, then south to windsurf-famous Vasiliki bay where the older kid can try a beginner lesson on the reliable afternoon wind.

Agios Nikitas village, Milos beach & Vasiliki bay

PhotosIG · agiosnikitasIG · milosbeachIG · vasilikiFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Agios Nikitas is Lefkada’s prettiest, car-free stone village — walkable lanes and easy tavernas. Just around the headland, Milos beach is reached on foot (15–20 min uphill each way, past an old windmill) or by a ~5-min water taxi (~€5–10pp) — the easier call with kids. Down south, Vasiliki bay is a windsurf mecca; the 13-year-old can try a beginner lesson on the reliable afternoon wind.

Village + bays

Village mornings, wind sports afternoons. Milos and the west side are calmest before midday; Vasiliki is the opposite — flat and calm early, then a dependable ~15–20kt thermal wind fills in around 3pm (which is exactly why windsurfers love it). Schedule family swims in the morning and any windsurf lesson for the afternoon.

Save Walk to Milos beach instead of the water taxi if the kids are up for the uphill return; wander Agios Nikitas and swim off the village beach for free.
Splurge A beginner windsurf lesson at Vasiliki for the older kid, and a sunset dinner in car-free Agios Nikitas.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • To Steki, Agios Nikitas — kids’ menu (grilled chicken/pasta), high chairs, and coloring books
  • Agios Nikitas village tavernas — simple fish, pasta, and grills in the car-free lanes
  • Vasiliki waterfront — casual lunch between windsurf sessions
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Water taxi to Milos to skip the hot uphill return.
  • Kids’ windsurf/SUP at Vasiliki on the afternoon wind.
  • Quiet village evening — Agios Nikitas is a lovely low-key dinner base.

Meganisi boat day

Sea caves, Skorpios & a shaded gorge

Est. $220 · boat cruise, lunch, dinner

SleepLefkada (Nydri) · night 4 of 5
AnchorNydri boat: Papanikolis cave + Skorpios swim stop
OptionDimosari gorge walk (low flow by June)

The Lefkada boat day from Nydri: motoring into the Papanikolis sea cave and around the old Onassis islands with turquoise swim stops — sheltered and gentle for the kids — with the shaded Dimosari gorge as a cool-off alternative if you want land time.

Nydri: Meganisi boat day & the Dimosari gorge

PhotosIG · nydriIG · meganisiIG · skorpiosFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

From Nydri harbour, a wooden day-boat cruises the Meganisi channel: the boat motors inside the Papanikolis sea cave (a real kid wow), then loops the former Onassis islands of Skorpios and Madouri with a turquoise swim stop — roughly €30–40pp for a group cruise, ~€70–80pp with lunch. Pair a morning gorge or afternoon boat: the free Dimosari gorge walk near Nydri is a short, shaded canyon (parking free), though the falls run low by June — treat it as a pretty walk, not a waterfall swim.

Boat + gorge

Calm channel, shaded gorge. The Meganisi/Skorpios cruise is sheltered and gentle — good for the 8-year-old — with warm ~73°F swim stops in turquoise coves. The Dimosari gorge is shaded and cool, a fine hot-afternoon alternative, but manage expectations on the waterfall flow by mid-June.

Save Take the group wooden-boat cruise (not a private charter) and bring your own snacks; the Dimosari gorge and its trailhead parking are free.
Splurge A smaller-group Meganisi cruise with lunch and more swim stops, or a private boat to time the Papanikolis cave without crowds.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Pomodoro, Nydri — wood-fired pizza and pasta on the marina, a picky-kid favourite
  • Nydri waterfront tavernas — grills and simple plates along the harbour
  • The Plane Tree, Dimosari trailhead — drinks/snacks at the gorge entrance
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Boat only if the gorge is dry — the Meganisi cruise is the real highlight.
  • Dimosari gorge morning, boat afternoon, or vice versa.
  • Sivota bay lunch by boat or car for another turquoise cove.

Lefkada Town + Gyra

An easy last day and repack

Est. $140 · town, lagoon, dinner

SleepLefkada (Nydri) · night 5 of 5
MorningLefkada Town, the causeway, the Gyra windmills
TonightRepack; Preveza airport is only ~20-36 min away

A relaxed finale: walkable Lefkada Town, the floating causeway, and the flat lagoon loop past the Gyra windmills, with a last waterfront dinner.

Lefkada Town, the lagoon & Gyra windmills

PhotosIG · lefkadatownIG · gyraIG · lefkadaFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

An easy, free last day. Lefkada Town connects to the mainland by the Agia Mavra floating causeway (no ferry ever needed to reach the island), and its centre is walkable and full of casual tavernas. The flat ~7km Gyra loop path rings the western lagoon past the surviving stone windmills and Gyra beach — a relaxed bike or drive-and-stroll to close the trip and repack before the short hop to Preveza airport.

Town + lagoon

Warm, flat, and shadeless around the lagoon. Walk or cycle the Gyra loop early or late; midday on the exposed spit is hot. The town itself has shaded lanes and a swing bridge that opens on the hour for boats — an easy, low-key finale after a busy Lefkada week.

Save The whole day is essentially free — walk the town, the lagoon path, and Gyra beach, and eat a final pizza in the centre.
Splurge A last seafood dinner on the Lefkada Town waterfront, or a sunset drink out at the Gyra windmills.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Made in Love, Lefkada Town — Neapolitan-trained pizzeria, family-friendly, gluten-free dough option
  • Lefkada Town centre tavernas — simple pasta, grills, and gyros in the walkable lanes
  • Bakeries + gelato — easy trailside snacks for the lagoon loop
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Gyra beach swim for one last dip near town.
  • Bike the lagoon loop instead of driving for an active last morning.
  • Early repack — Preveza airport is only ~20–36 min via the causeway.
