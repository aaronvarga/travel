Mon - 6/7/2027

Arrive Bonifacio by ferry

Change-of-scenery ferry beat

Est. ~$260 · ferry + meals

SleepBonifacio · night 1 of 2
PlanDrive 2h15 to Santa Teresa, 50-min ferry, Bonifacio evening

Cross the Strait of Bonifacio, settle near the old town, and keep the first evening focused on the cliff-top lanes and harbor.

Santa Teresa Gallura → Bonifacio ferry

PhotosIG · Santa Teresa GalluraIG · BonifacioFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

2026 route: 50-60 min. Typical family foot-passenger round trip budget €220-360; with car often €450-650+ round trip, and rental-company permission is the gating item.

June weather

78°F day / 63°F night. Sea 72-74°F — the crossing itself is short; beach swimming waits for Corsica.

Save Do not ferry the Sardinia rental unless the contract explicitly allows Italy-France ferry travel; park in Santa Teresa and go as foot passengers.
Splurge Pay for flex tickets and an earlier sailing so a windy Strait delay does not wreck the transfer day.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • La Bodega — traditional near citadel, €10-25 pp; Corsica Lovers flags it as child-suitable.
  • La Place — brasserie/pizza/burgers, ~€15-25 pp, 4.3★ TripAdvisor.
  • Le Bastion — family-rated old-town restaurant, ~€20-35 pp; fries and simple mains available.
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Maddalena day trip ↗ — stay in Sardinia and swap Corsica for La Maddalena if ferry prices or rental rules get ugly.
  • Maria Pia Beach ↗ — pine-shaded sand close to Alghero when old-town heat builds.
  • Bosa ↗ — painted riverside town about an hour south; a town-heavy alternate if wind spoils the beach.

Bonifacio clifftop old town

Cost

Old town free. King Aragon Steps often about €5 adult / €2.50 child; tourist train around €6-8 pp.

June weather

78°F day / 63°F night. Sea 72-74°F — good for swimming in nearby bays; old town is the town/history beat.

Save Sleep near the marina/port and skip a Corsica car for the town day; everything is walkable but steep.
Splurge Boat under the cliffs and sea caves, roughly €25-40 pp for the classic one-hour circuit.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Lavezzi Islands ↗ — boat-day alternative for clear water if beach parking is the problem.

Sat - 6/12/2027

Capo Caccia + Neptune's Grotto

Cliffs, cave, beach reset

Est. ~$190 · cave + meals

SleepAlghero · night 2 of 3
PlanCapo Caccia, Neptune's Grotto, Mugoni/Porto Conte swim

Do the cave early, then make the afternoon about water. If the sea is rough, use the stair route or skip the cave and beach-hop.

Capo Caccia & Neptune's Grotto

PhotosIG · Capo CacciaIG · Neptune's GrottoFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Viewpoints free. Neptune's Grotto: roughly €14 adult / €10 child plus boat or 654-step Escala del Cabirol.

June weather

79°F day / 64°F night. Sea 72-73°F — pleasant in sheltered coves, but the cave itself is not a swim stop.

Save Use the stairs if knees and heat are reasonable; skip boat fares and control timing.
Splurge Private dinghy or small-group snorkel around Capo Caccia, about €55-90 pp.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Fratelli Bizzarro — pizza/pasta, casual, ~€10-18 pp, strong pizza reviews; plain margherita works.
  • Faineria da Nino — budget faina/focaccia/pizza slices, ~€6-12 pp, easy snack dinner.
  • Mabrouk — set seafood/pasta menu for parents; kids can stick to simple pasta.
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Mugoni Beach ↗ — pine-backed, calmer family beach nearby when the cave feels too touristy.
  • Le Saline Beach ↗ — less famous and easier than La Pelosa while keeping clear water.
  • Maria Pia Beach ↗ — pine-shaded sand close to Alghero when old-town heat builds.

La Pelosa is the north-coast swim card. Book the slot once 2027 rules open; bring required mats under towels.

La Pelosa / Stintino beach day

PhotosIG · La PelosaIG · StintinoFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Timed-entry beach rules usually run June-Oct: cap around 1,500/day, ticket about €3.50 pp age 12+, mat under towels required; parking extra.

June weather

79°F day / 64°F night. Sea 72-73°F — shallow turquoise water is one of the best kid-swim setups in north Sardinia.

Save If La Pelosa slots are gone, use nearby Le Saline or Ezzi Mannu for free beach time.
Splurge Reserve loungers/umbrella early, roughly €35-60/day, to avoid fighting for shade.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Fratelli Bizzarro — pizza/pasta, casual, ~€10-18 pp, strong pizza reviews; plain margherita works.
  • Faineria da Nino — budget faina/focaccia/pizza slices, ~€6-12 pp, easy snack dinner.
  • Mabrouk — set seafood/pasta menu for parents; kids can stick to simple pasta.
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Le Saline Beach ↗ — less famous, easier parking, still clear water and room for kids to spread out.
  • Maria Pia Beach ↗ — pine-shaded sand close to Alghero when old-town heat builds.
  • Bosa ↗ — painted riverside town about an hour south; a town-heavy alternate if wind spoils the beach.

Mon - 6/14/2027

Cala Goloritze hike + swim

Signature hike + real swim

Est. ~$130 · permits + meals

SleepOgliastra · night 2 of 4
PlanEarly Cala Goloritze hike, beach swim, slow dinner

This is the hardest day in the plan. Moving one day earlier keeps the same weather band, but it makes the early start even more important for heat and kid patience.

Cala Goloritze hike / booking day

AllTrails
PhotosIG · Cala GoloritzeFlickrAllTrails
1 / 3
Cost

2026-style rules: book ahead via Heart of Sardinia; typical hike ticket €7 pp, daily cap around 250, trail opens 7:30 and closes 14:00; beach stay until late afternoon.

June weather

81°F day / 66°F night. Sea 73-75°F — clear and swimmable, but the hike out is hot; start early with the 8-year-old.

Save Book the hike instead of a boat landing; the trail ticket is far cheaper and avoids boat crowds.
Splurge Hire a local guide for the hike or switch to a small-group Gulf of Orosei boat if heat spikes.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • MeC Puddu's — Santa Maria Navarrese pizzeria/Italian, ~€10-20 pp; pizza, pasta, fries.
  • Ristorante Il Pozzo — Italian/pizza/seafood, ~€12-25 pp; reviewers mention pizza and hungry-kid friendliness.
  • Shardana — pizzeria/Italian, ~€12-22 pp; good fallback for plain pizza and pasta.
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Cala Mariolu by boat ↗ — same Gulf of Orosei wow water with less hiking; book landing QR rules if required.
  • Cala Luna ↗ — larger beach with caves, easier for a longer sit if kids tire of moving.
  • Supramonte viewpoint picnic ↗ — nature scenery without committing to a long canyon hike.

Drive Ogliastra → Cagliari

City + beach convenience

Est. ~$150 · fuel + dinner

SleepCagliari · night 1 of 3
Plan2h drive, Castello, Bastione, easy dinner

Cagliari gives the trip a town-and-food finish while keeping Poetto beach minutes away.

Cagliari Castello & Bastione

PhotosIG · Cagliari CastelloIG · BastioneFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Old town free. Bastione free; towers/museums usually €3-10 pp. Parking garages near Marina/Castello €1.50-2.50/hr.

June weather

84°F day / 68°F night. Sea 73-75°F — south coast is reliably warm enough for kid swimming.

Save Use one paid garage and walk the old town instead of chasing street parking in heat.
Splurge Rooftop aperitivo or guided food walk, about €40-75 pp for adults; kids can split pizza after.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Pizzeria Cerere — top family-pizza pick, ~€10-18 pp, plain pizza safety net.
  • Bombas — local burgers/fries, ~€10-18 pp; the burger stop for the picky kid.
  • Caprizza Cagliari — family-friendly pizzeria, ~€10-20 pp; easy Marina/Castello dinner.
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Molentargius flamingo park ↗ — flat bike/walk with flamingos if the old town feels too hot.
  • Chia beaches ↗ — dune-backed beach payoff west of Pula if ruins get voted down.
  • Nora ruins ↗ — coastal ruins plus Pula gelato if you want history with beach access nearby.
