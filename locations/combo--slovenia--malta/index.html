Tue - 6/8/2027

Lake Bled

Island church, pletna, and the clifftop castle

Est. $200 · boat, island, castle, dinner

SleepLake Bled · night 2 of 4
AnchorPletna to Bled Island + Bled Castle
TreatBled cream cake (kremšnita) on a castle terrace

The postcard day: a pletna boat to the island church and wishing bell, then up to the clifftop castle for the view down the lake. The iconic Slovenia image, and an easy one for the kids.

Lake Bled: island church, pletna & clifftop castle

PhotosIG · lakebledIG · bledislandIG · sloveniaFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

The pletna boat to the island is €20 adult / €10 child round trip (cash to the pletnar, ~20 min each way plus ~40 min on the island); the island church admission with the wishing bell is €12 adult / €5 child. Bled Castle is about €19 adult / €7 child. A family of four doing all three runs roughly €150 (~$165). Walking the lake shore, ringing nothing, and photographing the island is free.

Lake

~1,500 ft, Julian Alps foothills. June highs are a pleasant ~24°C / mid-70s°F with frequent afternoon showers. The lake itself is a swimmable-but-cool ~18–19°C in June — fine for a quick dip off the grassy lidos, not a warm-water day (that waits for Piran).

Save Skip the pletna and walk the 6 km lakeshore path for the classic island view for free; ring the wishing bell only if the kids insist. The castle courtyard view is the same whether or not you buy the museum ticket.
Splurge Do the full ritual: pletna across, 99 steps up, ring the bell, then a slice of Bled cream cake (kremšnita) on a castle terrace over the lake.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Pizzeria Rustika — wood-fired pizza with a kids’ pizza noted and simple pasta, a reliable picky-eater anchor
  • Pizzeria Gallus — big straightforward pizzas near the lake
  • Slaščičarna Šmon — the original Bled cream cake plus ice cream for a low-drama treat stop
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Bled Castle first, island after if morning light is better on the water later.
  • Straž hill or Ojstrica viewpoint for the postcard aerial of the island without a drone.
  • Rent a rowboat instead of the pletna for a cheaper, more active crossing.

Wed - 6/9/2027

Vintgar Gorge

Emerald boardwalk through the canyon

Est. $150 · gorge entry, lunch, dinner

SleepLake Bled · night 3 of 4
AnchorVintgar Gorge (book the online timed slot)
Weather ruleShaded and cool - a good warm-or-showery-morning pick

The 1.6 km wooden boardwalk clings to the emerald Radovna over rapids to the Šum waterfall. Buy the timed online pass in advance — there are no tickets at the gate anymore — and go early to beat the buses.

Vintgar Gorge: emerald boardwalk through the Radovna canyon

PhotosIG · vintgargorgeIG · blejskivintgarIG · sloveniaFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Entry is €15 adult / €5 child (up to 15) — about €40 (~$44) for the family. Since 2025 there are NO tickets at the gorge entrance: you must buy an online timed-slot pass in advance (or on-site only at the P1 Vintgar central parking before the shuttle). Miss your slot and you wait. Open April–October only; helmets are provided and mandatory.

Gorge

Shaded river canyon. The 1.6 km wooden boardwalk hugs the emerald Radovna over rapids and pools, ending at the Šum waterfall. Cool and often damp underfoot — a smart pick on a warm or showery morning. About 45 minutes one way; fine for both kids, not for strollers.

Save Book the earliest online slot to beat the tour buses and the midday crush, and walk back the upper forest loop rather than paying for the shuttle.
Splurge Pair it with the nearby St Catherine’s church hilltop or the Pokljuka plateau for a bigger half-day in the Bled backcountry.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Gostilna Vintgar (at the entrance) — simple grilled plates, fries, and pizza right by the exit
  • Pizzeria Rustika (Bled, ~10 min) — the dependable kids’-pizza fallback back in town
  • Bled lakeside kiosks — ice cream and quick bites after the walk
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Come at opening or late afternoon — midday is the crowd peak on the narrow boardwalk.
  • Tolmin or Great Soča gorges later if you want a second, different canyon on the Soča leg.
  • Skip if heavy rain closes or slicks the boardwalk — it’s a fair-weather walk.

Thu - 6/10/2027

Lake Bohinj + Vogel

Wilder lake, cable-car panorama, waterfall

Est. $210 · cable car, Savica, dinner

SleepLake Bled · night 4 of 4
MorningVogel cable car over Triglav National Park
AfternoonSavica Waterfall + Bohinj lakeshore

Quieter and wilder than Bled, deep in Triglav National Park: the Vogel cable car for the big peak panorama, the Savica waterfall walk, and a glassy lake for a kayak. Pack a layer for the summit and repack tonight for the Vršič crossing.

Lake Bohinj: Vogel cable car + Savica Waterfall

PhotosIG · lakebohinjIG · vogelIG · triglavnationalparkFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Quieter and wilder than Bled, inside Triglav National Park (no park entry fee). The Vogel cable car is about €33 adult / €16 child return for a huge balcony view over the lake to the Triglav massif. Savica Waterfall is a ~€4 walk-in on a stepped path. Kayak or SUP rental on the lake runs ~€15–20/hour. The lakeshore and the stone bridge at Ribčev Laz are free.

Alpine lake

Glacial lake, deeper in the park. Bohinj is colder than Bled — high-teens °C water even in June — and can catch mountain cloud. The Vogel top station sits above 1,500 m, so pack a layer even on a warm valley day.

Save Do the free lakeshore walk and the Savica path, and skip the cable car if the summit is clouded — the view is the whole point of the ticket.
Splurge Ride Vogel on a clear morning for the Triglav panorama, then kayak the glassy east end of the lake in the afternoon.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Pizzerija Ema — very family-friendly with a small playground and coloring for kids, wood-fired pizza
  • Pizzeria Ukanc — large simple pizzas at the quiet Ukanc (west) end of the lake
  • Slaščičarna at Ribčev Laz — ice cream and pancakes by the bridge
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Savica + lakeshore only as a gentle half-day if the peaks are socked in.
  • Mostnica Gorge (Stara Fužina) for a shorter, cheaper canyon walk with a cow-shaped rock the kids hunt for.
  • Panoramic lake boat instead of the cable car for a low-effort option.

Fri - 6/11/2027

Vršič Pass to the Soča Valley

Slovenia’s highest road, 50 hairpins

Est. $120 · picnic, hut stop, dinner

SleepBovec / Soča Valley · night 1 of 3
AnchorVršič Pass (1611 m), Russian Chapel, Lake Jasna
Risk checkConfirm the pass is open on promet.si; Predel is the fallback

The scenic transfer that earns its own day: over the Vršič Pass’s 50 numbered hairpins, past the WWI Russian Chapel and turquoise Lake Jasna, down to the emerald Soča. Check the pass is clear before setting off; if late snow has it shut, loop via Predel/Tarvisio.

Scenic transfer - Bled to Bovec over Vršič Pass (or Predel if closed).

Vršič Pass: 50 hairpins over the roof of Slovenia

PhotosIG · vrsicpassIG · julianalpsIG · socavalleyFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Free to drive — the pass is a regional mountain road, so no vignette or toll applies. The only costs are fuel and your nerves on 50 numbered hairpins (24 up from Kranjska Gora, 26 down to the Soča). Budget the whole day for it: the Russian Chapel, the summit, Lake Jasna, and a dozen pull-offs.

High pass

1,611 m — Slovenia’s highest road pass. Normally fully open and clear by mid-June, but this is the one real timing risk of the trip: late-lying snow can delay the spring clearing and there is no guaranteed open-by date. Check promet.si and the Erjavčeva koča cameras before committing; the Predel/Tarvisio route is the fallback if it’s shut.

Save It’s a free scenic drive — pack a picnic for the summit and let the road be the day’s main event rather than paying for anything up top.
Splurge Stop at Erjavčeva koča near the top for štruklji and a hot chocolate with the peaks overhead, and detour to turquoise Lake Jasna on the descent.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Erjavčeva koča (near the summit) — mountain-hut plates, štruklji, soups, and simple kid food with a view
  • Okrepčevalnica Loka (Trenta) — casual grill and pizza on the Soča side of the pass
  • Pack a picnic — the smartest move for the summit and the pull-offs
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Predel Pass via Tarvisio (Italy) as the ~2–2.5 hr fallback if Vršič is closed — still Schengen, no border stop.
  • Lake Jasna (Kranjska Gora side) for an easy turquoise-water photo stop with the ibex statue.
  • Soča Trail / river source (Trenta) for a short leg-stretch on the way down.

Sat - 6/12/2027

Soča Valley water day

Emerald river, rafting, the Great Gorge

Est. $320 · rafting, gorge, dinner

SleepBovec / Soča Valley · night 2 of 3
AnchorFamily rafting + Great Soča Gorge + Kozjak Waterfall
Water truthSoča is a glacial 11-15°C - wetsuits, not casual swimming

The adventure heart of the trip: guided rafting on the impossibly green Soča (a gentle family stretch for the 8-year-old, the standard run for the 13-year-old), then the free Great Soča Gorge and the easy Kozjak Waterfall walk. Book the water for the morning ahead of afternoon storms.

Soča Valley: emerald river, family rafting & the Great Gorge

PhotosIG · socariverIG · bovecIG · raftingFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

The impossibly emerald Soča is the heart of the leg. Guided rafting runs roughly €45–80/person plus a ~€6 river permit; standard trips take ages 8+ (the 13-year-old is fine), while a dedicated family/panoramic float suits the 8-year-old. The Great Soča Gorge (Velika korita) has free parking and no entry fee; Kozjak Waterfall is €5 adult / €3 child on an easy 3.3 km forest walk.

River

Glacial river, cold year-round. The Soča is a stunning ~11–15°C even in summer — perfect for wetsuit rafting, too cold for casual kid swimming beyond a shriek-and-out dip. June valley air is warm; afternoon thunderstorms are common, so book water sports for the morning.

Save Do the free Great Soča Gorge boardwalk and the cheap Kozjak Waterfall walk, and pick the shorter family rafting stretch rather than the full-day trip.
Splurge Book the guided rafting for the whole family (wetsuits included) and add the Boka waterfall viewpoint or a via-ferrata taster for the 13-year-old.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Pizzeria Črna Ovca (“Black Sheep”), Bovec — widely praised oven-baked pizza, English-speaking staff
  • Ristorante Pizzeria Papillo, Bovec — family-friendly and fast, plain pizza/pasta guaranteed
  • Letni Vrt, Bovec — pizza-focused garden spot for an easy dinner
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Family/panoramic raft (Čezsoča stretch) for the 8-year-old instead of the standard run.
  • Great Soča Gorge + Kozjak as a no-rafting, low-cost water day.
  • Kobarid WWI museum + Napoleon Bridge for a rainy-morning indoor pivot.

Sun - 6/13/2027

Tolmin Gorges

Devil’s Bridge and the park’s lowest canyon

Est. $150 · gorge entry, lunch, dinner

SleepBovec / Soča Valley · night 3 of 3
AnchorTolmin Gorges (timed 1-hour slot)
OptionBovec zipline / Kobarid WWI museum add-ons

A cooler canyon walk to close the Soča leg: the Devil’s Bridge high over the river, the thermal spring at the confluence, and Dante’s Cave. Add the Bovec zipline for the 13-year-old (height/weight limits apply) or the Kobarid museum if it rains.

Tolmin Gorges: Devil’s Bridge & the Triglav park’s lowest canyon

PhotosIG · tolmingorgesIG · tolminskakoritaIG · socavalleyFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Entry is €12 adult / €6 child (6–15) in high season — about €36 (~$40) for the family — on a one-hour timed slot. The loop takes in the Devil’s Bridge (60 m above the river), the thermal spring at the Tolminka–Zadlaščica confluence, and Dante’s Cave. Open roughly April–October; it closes only in exceptional high water, and tickets are refundable if it does.

Gorge

Lowest entry point into Triglav National Park. A cooler, shaded canyon walk — a good hot-afternoon pick. The narrow paths and stairs are fine for both kids but not for strollers. After heavy rain the Tolminka can rise and force a closure, so keep it weather-flexible.

Save It’s already cheap — just go early for your slot and bring water; the thermal-spring pool is a free cool-off.
Splurge Add the nearby Javorca memorial church or a Tolmin town gelato, or combine with the Kozjak Waterfall for a two-canyon day.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Pizzeria Cinca Marinca, Tolmin — casual pizza and pasta in town
  • Gostilna Rajht (Kobarid, ~20 min) — hearty local plates with simpler kid options
  • Tolmin bakeries — burek and pastries for a cheap trail lunch
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Great Soča Gorge instead if you’d rather a free, shorter canyon walk.
  • Tolmin thermal spring pool for a quick kid cool-off inside the gorge.
  • Skip in high water — check the gorge’s status line before driving over.

Mon - 6/14/2027

Drive to the coast via Škocjan Caves

Underground canyon, then the Adriatic

Est. $180 · cave tour, transfer, dinner

SleepPiran · night 1 of 3
AnchorŠkocjan Caves (UNESCO) en route to Piran
TransferBovec -> Škocjan -> Piran ~2h30 total

The Alps-to-coast transition, broken by the UNESCO Škocjan Caves — a vast underground canyon crossed by a footbridge high over the Reka. It sits right on the route to Piran, so it’s a marquee stop, not a detour. Arrive on the coast for the first warm-water evening.

Scenic transfer - Bovec to Piran with the Škocjan Caves en route.

Škocjan Caves: an underground canyon bridged high over the Reka

PhotosIG · skocjancavesIG · skocjanskejameIG · unescoFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

The UNESCO show-stopper on the drive to the coast. The Underground Canyon tour is about €22–24 adult / €12.50 child in summer — roughly €70 (~$77) for the family — with departures around 10:00, 11:30, 13:00, 14:00, 15:00. It’s a 5 km, ~1,000-step, 2.5–3 hr walk crossing the famous Cerkvenik footbridge high above the Reka. Interior is ~12°C; no photography inside.

Cave

~12°C underground, year-round. Bring a layer even on a hot coastal day. This is a genuinely strenuous walk, not a stroll, and not stroller-accessible — fine for a capable 8-year-old but a real hike. The early-exit option at the Big Collapse Doline shortens it to ~3 km.

Save Take the standard Underground Canyon tour rather than any combo add-ons; the collapse-doline early exit trims the steps if legs are tired.
Splurge If the 8-year-old would prefer easier magic, swap to Postojna Cave’s ride-in cave train — less dramatic than Škocjan’s canyon, but stroller-adaptable and a kid hit.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Gostilna near the park entrance — simple grilled plates and pizza before the drive on
  • Divača bakeries (~10 min) — burek and pastries for a car snack
  • Piran/Portorož dinner — save the real meal for arrival on the coast
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Postojna Cave as the easier, train-served alternative for younger kids.
  • Predjama Castle combined with Postojna if you pivot inland.
  • Big Collapse Doline early exit to cut the tour short without missing the canyon.

Tue - 6/15/2027

Piran old town

Venetian lanes and the first Adriatic swim

Est. $150 · walls, bell tower, taverna

SleepPiran · night 2 of 3
AnchorTartini Square, town walls, St George’s bell tower
PayoffSea ~22°C / 72°F - the warm-water leg the trip builds to

The reward after the mountains: a Venetian peninsula of red roofs and narrow lanes, the town-wall panorama, and the first proper Adriatic swim off the Punta rocks. Warmer and calmer than the Atlantic plans, if honestly a refreshing 72°F rather than tropical.

Piran: a Venetian peninsula and the first warm Adriatic swim

PhotosIG · piranIG · slovenianistriaIG · adriaticFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Wandering Tartini Square, the harbor, and the tangle of Venetian lanes is free. The town walls are €3 adult / €2 student (under-12 free) for the best red-roof panorama; St George’s bell tower is ~€3 to climb its 146 steps. Piran’s own shoreline is rocky bathing platforms with ladders into deep water — the warm-water payoff of the whole trip, if a cooler-than-tropical one.

Coast

Northern Adriatic. June air is a warm ~25–28°C / high-70s°F, and the sea is about ~22°C / 72°F — genuinely swimmable but refreshing, not bathwater, especially early June. This is warmer and calmer than the Atlantic beaches on the Portugal-style plans, and the honest ceiling on this trip’s swimming.

Save Swim free off the Punta rocks or the concrete platforms along Prešernovo nabrežje, and climb the €3 walls at golden hour instead of a pricier boat trip.
Splurge A sunset seafood dinner on the Piran waterfront, then the bell-tower climb for the 360° over the peninsula as the lights come on.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Pizzeria Porto Konoba — among the best pizza on the Slovenian coast, plus simple seafood
  • Pizzeria Burin — straightforward pizza with a sea view and family-friendly staff
  • Fritolin pri Cantini — casual order-at-the-counter fried calamari and simple plates at fair prices
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Fiesa beach (15-min walk) for easier pebble-beach family swimming.
  • Piran town walls at sunset for the red-roof panorama without midday heat.
  • Boat or kayak to the cliffs for a calm-water paddle off the peninsula.

This is the water-temperature fix in its purest form. Book the new free Blue Lagoon access pass before you go.

Blue Lagoon + Comino

PhotosIG · Blue LagoonIG · CominoFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

The 2026 visitor system requires a free pre-booked Blue Lagoon pass to step on land, with morning/afternoon/evening slots; commercial boat trips commonly ~€30–45 adult / child discounts.

June weather

Air ~80–84°F / 69–71°F. Comino water should be around 73–74°F on 6/21/2027, excellent compared with Portugal and usually calmer/warmer-feeling inside the lagoon.

Save Book the free slot yourself before buying a tour; choose the morning slot for clearer water and fewer boats.
Splurge Private or semi-private Comino/Gozo boat, ~€250–450 family, avoids packed party boats.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • I Pupi Pizzeria Valletta — excellent-value pizza, 4.9★, ~$12–20 pp

  • Sally Port Pizzeria — pizza/pasta in Valletta, 4.8★; familiar dinner fallback
