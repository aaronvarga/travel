Cascais waterfront + Boca do Inferno

PhotosIG · CascaisIG · Boca do InfernoFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Waterfront, beaches, and Boca do Inferno are free. Lisbon-Cascais train is roughly EUR2-3 each way; taxi/transfer from LIS to Cascais often lands around $65-$110.

Weather

June: about 73-76 F days / 59-64 F nights. Atlantic water is chilly, roughly 62-64 F, so treat it as scenery and quick dips, not the main swim act.

Save Stay in Cascais/Estoril and use train/taxi instead of renting a Portugal car for only two nights.
Splurge Private airport transfer to Cascais after the overnight flight; worth the ~$65-$110 if everyone is tired.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Na Pizza Cascais — Margherita listed at EUR13; easy plain pizza night
  • Pasta Non Basta Cascais — fresh pasta and pizza, simple tomato/cheese fallback
  • Passione Pizza Sintra — Sintra pizza stop with plain kid-friendly orders
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Lisbon Oceanarium ↩ — excellent bad-weather or jet-lag backup; current tickets are EUR25 adults/13+, EUR15 ages 3-12.
  • Monserrate Palace ↗ — lush gardens and lighter crowds than Pena; good if timed palace slots are gone.
  • Praia da Rainha ↗ — tiny in-town cove for a quick swim/photo when the open Atlantic is rough.

Sintra: Quinta da Regaleira + Praia da Adraga

PhotosIG · SintraIG · Quinta da RegaleiraIG · Praia da AdragaFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Quinta da Regaleira official 2026 pricing: EUR15 adult, EUR10 youth 6-17 and seniors 65+, under 6 free. There is no combined family ticket, so a family of 4 (two adults + two kids) is about EUR50. Pena Palace is timed-entry and more crowd-sensitive.

Weather

June: Sintra is cooler than Lisbon and can be misty; Adraga beach is dramatic but Atlantic-cold.

Save Choose Regaleira over a full Pena interior day; the tunnels and Initiation Well are more kid-magical and the pricing is clear (about EUR50 for the four of us).
Splurge Add Pena Palace only if you can secure an early timed slot; current rules require booking the exact day/time for palace entry.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Na Pizza Cascais — Margherita listed at EUR13; easy plain pizza night
  • Pasta Non Basta Cascais — fresh pasta and pizza, simple tomato/cheese fallback
  • Passione Pizza Sintra — Sintra pizza stop with plain kid-friendly orders
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Pena Palace park/terraces ↩ — more iconic color, but stricter timed entry and more crowd pressure.
  • Monserrate Palace ↗ — lush gardens and lighter crowds than Pena if the main Sintra palaces are slammed.
  • Praia da Rainha ↗ — tiny Cascais cove for an easier swim/photo if the Sintra weather is poor.

Lisbon: Belém, Alfama & Tram 28

PhotosIG · LisbonIG · BelémFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

City wandering is free. Belém Tower and Jerónimos Monastery are timed-entry, roughly EUR8-15 adult each with reduced/free kids; Tram 28 is a normal transit ride (~EUR3) or on a 24h Viva Viagem pass.

Weather

June: Lisbon runs warm, about 77-84 F and hotter than the coast. Do Alfama and Belém earlier, then riverside gelato and shade in the afternoon.

Save Ride the real Tram 28 as your sightseeing instead of a paid hop-on bus, and use a reloadable Viva Viagem card for the family.
Splurge Pastéis de Belém from the original bakery, or the Lisbon Oceanarium if a kid needs an indoor reset.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Na Pizza Cascais — Margherita listed at EUR13; easy plain pizza night
  • Pasta Non Basta Cascais — fresh pasta and pizza, simple tomato/cheese fallback
  • Passione Pizza Sintra — Sintra pizza stop with plain kid-friendly orders
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Lisbon Oceanarium ↩ — excellent heat/meltdown backup; current tickets EUR25 adults, EUR15 ages 3-12.
  • Pena Palace park ↗ — colorful terraces and forested grounds when the full interior visit feels too crowded.
  • Praia das Macas ↗ — bigger family beach with food nearby if cliff coves feel too exposed.

Cabo da Roca, Praia da Ursa & Azenhas do Mar

PhotosIG · Cabo da RocaIG · Praia da UrsaFlickr
1 / 2
Cost

Free. Cabo da Roca (mainland Europe’s westernmost point) has free parking and viewpoints; Praia da Ursa is a steep unofficial scramble (not for little kids alone); Azenhas do Mar has a natural sea pool below the village.

Weather

June: sunny but windy and Atlantic-cold water. Great for views and tide pools, not long swims — that is what Sicily is for.

Save Pack a picnic — there is little food at Cabo da Roca beyond one café, and the views beat overpriced snacks.
Splurge A cliffside lunch or sunset drink at Azenhas do Mar, or a short Sintra-coast e-bike stretch.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Na Pizza Cascais — Margherita listed at EUR13; easy plain pizza night
  • Pasta Non Basta Cascais — fresh pasta and pizza, simple tomato/cheese fallback
  • Passione Pizza Sintra — Sintra pizza stop with plain kid-friendly orders
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Praia da Adraga / Guincho ↩ — easier sand-and-café beaches if the Ursa scramble is too much.
  • Praia das Macas ↗ — bigger family beach with food nearby if cliff coves feel too exposed.
  • Lisbon Oceanarium ↗ — reliable bad-weather or jet-lag backup before the Sicily flight.

Blue Lagoon + Comino

PhotosIG · Blue LagoonIG · CominoFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

The 2026 visitor system requires a free pre-booked Blue Lagoon pass to step on land, with morning/afternoon/evening slots; commercial boat trips commonly ~€30–45 adult / child discounts.

June weather

Air ~80–84°F / 69–71°F. Comino water should be around 73–74°F on 6/21/2027, excellent compared with Portugal and usually calmer/warmer-feeling inside the lagoon.

Save Book the free slot yourself before buying a tour; choose the morning slot for clearer water and fewer boats.
Splurge Private or semi-private Comino/Gozo boat, ~€250–450 family, avoids packed party boats.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • I Pupi Pizzeria Valletta — excellent-value pizza, 4.9★, ~$12–20 pp
  • Casa Sotto — Roman pizza in Valletta, top family pizza pick
  • Sally Port Pizzeria — pizza/pasta in Valletta, 4.8★; familiar dinner fallback
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Għajn Tuffieħa ↗ — Malta beach day with sand and no Comino boat logistics.
  • Ghajn Tuffieha ↗ — proper sandy beach day without Comino boat logistics.
  • Ramla Bay ↗ — red-sand beach version of a Gozo day if a full island loop is too much.

Gozo day trip

PhotosIG · GozoFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Gozo Channel return foot passenger €4.65 adult; children 3–12 €1.15; pay on return from Gozo. Fast ferry from Valletta is more, about €8.50 one way.

June weather

Air ~80–84°F / 69–71°F. Xlendi/Ramla water should be around 73–75°F on 6/22/2027.

Save Go as foot passengers and use a Gozo taxi/Bolt for key stops; taking a Malta rental car over adds cost and parking friction.
Splurge Private Gozo driver for 5–6 hrs, ~€180–280, lets you hit Victoria, Ramla, salt pans and Xlendi without bus waits.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Country Terrace — views near Mgarr; pasta, pizza-ish kids choices, ~$18–30 pp
  • Maldonado Bistro — Victoria bistro with pasta/burgers, ~$15–25 pp
  • Front Bar Xlendi — casual seafront, burgers/fries/sandwiches
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Ramla Bay ↗ — make it a red-sand beach day instead of a full island loop.
  • Three Cities waterfront ↗ — harbor views and old streets with a calmer feel than Valletta.
  • Popeye Village viewpoint ↗ — kitschy but kid-friendly photo stop near a scenic cove.
