The Plan at a Glance

Maui polish, Kauai drama

A full-length Hawaii alternative built around scenery and warm water—not a three-island checklist.

5 nights

Kīhei, Maui

Molokini-or-reef snorkeling, Haleakalā, one Hāna road day and a protected recovery day.

4 nights

Poʻipū, Kauai

The right side for Port Allen, Waimea/Kōkeʻe and the trip’s reliable lifeguarded beach anchor.

Hāʻena and Hanalei without two hours of daily cross-island backtracking.

Recommendation: This scenery-first Hawaii plan enters at #13 with 32/50, tying the existing Oʻahu + Hawaiʻi Island route on raw score. It buys 12 nights and the board’s strongest tropical landscape sequence, but Maui is a repeat and the realistic $14.6k–21.6k band makes it the more expensive Hawaii choice.

Why This Trip

The premium scenery version of Hawaii

It earns a separate comparison slot because it answers a different family question.

What it does best

Warm water + enormous scenery

  • Haleakalā, Hāna, Waimea Canyon, Nā Pali and Hanalei form a visually exceptional sequence.
  • Twelve nights correct the existing Hawaii plan’s length penalty.

Why not combine all four islands

One hop is enough

  • Two rentals, one OGG→LIH flight and three bases are already a moderate logistics load.
  • Oʻahu or Hawaiʻi Island would turn the plan into airport sampling.

What keeps it from winning

Visible penalties

  • The audited band is $14.6k–21.6k; only the low edge touches the family preference.
  • Maui is already visited, so novelty is 2/5.
  • Hāʻena, Nā Pali and two long Maui drives add weather/reservation fragility.

Upcountry + Haleakalā daylight and sunset

PhotosIG · haleakalaIG · mauisunsetsFlickr
1 / 5
Cost

The current private-vehicle entrance is $30, cashless and valid three days. Sunset needs no sunrise ticket; food, warm clothing and gas are unavailable in the park.

Summit reality

Beach weather does not apply at 10,023 feet. NPS warns dawn and dusk can be wet, windy and below freezing; the road lacks streetlights and guardrails.

Save Drive up in daylight, walk short overlooks, watch sunset only if visibility and family energy justify the dark descent, and bring winter layers.
Splurge A guided summit tour is worthwhile only if nobody wants the long drive back; sunrise would add a 3–7am reservation and an unhealthy 2am start.

The long-drive day

The official route has 620 curves and 59 bridges; Kahului to Hāna is 52 miles and can take 2–4 hours before sightseeing. Rain and flash flooding can change the day quickly.

Save Reserve one state-park slot 30 days out and cap the day at Hāna/Waiʻānapanapa. Do not force Pīpīwai from Kīhei.

Two high-output drives, separated

Weather buffer before moving

Reservations outrank wish lists
