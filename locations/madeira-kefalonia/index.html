The Case

Atlantic drama, then Ionian calm

A 12-night two-island hybrid with six full nights on each island and no Lefkada/PVK dependency.

What Madeira addsReal ridge and levada days, Fanal’s mist forest, volcanic pools and cinematic coastal driving — not just a Portugal gateway.
What Kefalonia fixesWarmer, calmer Greek swimming and short island drives without the ferry/thin-exit exposure of the Kefalonia + Lefkada plan.
What the ticket protectsFNC in and EFL out via Athens: avoid Preveza and keep the Atlantic/Greek backbone on protected, rebookable tickets.

Why This Hybrid

The Madeira + Crete alternative with a simpler Greek finish

Warm-calm over ferry complexity

Kefalonia supplies Myrtos, Assos, Melissani, Antisamos, Fiskardo, Argostoli/Lassi and the Zakynthos boat in one island rental-car loop. No Lefkada transfer and no Preveza exit are required.

Athens is the protection point

The tested Atlantic + Madeira feed remains PIT–EWR–LIS–FNC. The pivot is Athens: current Aegean ATH–EFL service is a frequent proxy, so return through ATH rather than a thin charter/self-transfer airport.

A cleaner family mix

Madeira takes the hike/scenery burden; Kefalonia takes the swim burden. That makes Myrtos a photo/wade stop and keeps truly easy swims at Antisamos and Lassi.

Two Bases

Six nights each, no buffer hotel

Book apartment-first: AC, parking, kitchen, laundry, two real sleeping zones and free cancellation.

Entry & Documents

Passports and essentials

Documents, driving, money, health, and connectivity for this trip. Time-sensitive items (especially ETIAS) can change — re-check the linked official sources close to departure.

Portugal (incl. Madeira)

PassportValid ≥3 months beyond your planned EU departure, and issued within the last 10 years.
ETIASScheduled to begin operations in the last quarter of 2026; likely mandatory for eligible visa-exempt visitors by June 2027, but recheck the official launch date. — €20 per eligible traveler (about $22 at planning exchange rates; subject to change). Under-18s and over-70s are exempt.
VisaNone for US citizens — visa-free up to 90 days per 180.
Driving permitRecommended, not legally required. Get one from AAA before you go; some rental insurers ask for it.
Rental carsRental fleet is mostly manual — book an automatic early and expect a premium. Min age ~21; under-25 surcharge ~€6–15/day.
TollsElectronic tolls (Via Verde). Rentals usually include a transponder (~€1–2/day + tolls); confirm toll handling when booking.
MoneyCards widely accepted in hotels/restaurants/shops. Carry €50–100 — small cafés and family spots may be cash/Multibanco only.
TippingNot expected; 5–10% appreciated for good service (cash).
Tap waterSafe in Lisbon and most cities. Algarve tap can taste earthy (hard water) — bottled if sensitive.
Emergency112 (EU-wide, free, mobile or landline).
ConnectivityHolafly or Airalo Eurolink work well; buy before departure.
ETIAS is a pre-travel authorization for visa-free visitors incl. US citizens. The official EU launch timing has shifted before, so reconfirm rules, fee, and eligibility close to departure.

Sources: US State Dept — Schengen passport rules · EU — ETIAS official · Via Verde visitors

Tipping10–15% for good restaurant service; round up casual bills (cash).
Tap waterAthens/mainland excellent; Crete tap is safe but very hard (chalky). Some smaller islands need bottled.
Emergency112 (EU-wide). Also ambulance 166.
ConnectivityHolafly or Airalo Eurolink work well; buy before departure.
ETIAS is a pre-travel authorization for visa-free visitors incl. US citizens. The official EU launch timing has shifted before, so reconfirm rules, fee, and eligibility close to departure.
