Portugal starts the trip with scenery, pastries, tiled streets, and an easy coast, not a hard city day.

Cascais waterfront + Boca do Inferno

PhotosIG · CascaisIG · Boca do InfernoFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Waterfront, beaches, and Boca do Inferno are free. Lisbon-Cascais train is roughly EUR2-3 each way; taxi/transfer from LIS to Cascais often lands around $65-$110.

Weather

June: about 73-76 F days / 59-64 F nights. Atlantic water is chilly, roughly 62-64 F, so treat it as scenery and quick dips, not the main swim act.

Save Stay in Cascais/Estoril and use train/taxi instead of renting a Portugal car for only two nights.
Splurge Private airport transfer to Cascais after the overnight flight; worth the ~$65-$110 if everyone is tired.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Na Pizza Cascais — Margherita listed at EUR13; easy plain pizza night
  • Pasta Non Basta Cascais — fresh pasta and pizza, simple tomato/cheese fallback
  • Passione Pizza Sintra — Sintra pizza stop with plain kid-friendly orders
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Lisbon Oceanarium ↩ — excellent bad-weather or jet-lag backup; current tickets are EUR25 adults/13+, EUR15 ages 3-12.
  • Monserrate Palace ↗ — lush gardens and lighter crowds than Pena; good if timed palace slots are gone.
  • Praia da Rainha ↗ — tiny in-town cove for a quick swim/photo when the open Atlantic is rough.

Sintra: Quinta da Regaleira + Praia da Adraga

PhotosIG · SintraIG · Quinta da RegaleiraIG · Praia da AdragaFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Quinta da Regaleira official 2026 pricing: EUR15 adult, EUR10 youth 6-17 and seniors 65+, under 6 free. There is no combined family ticket, so a family of 4 (two adults + two kids) is about EUR50. Pena Palace is timed-entry and more crowd-sensitive.

Weather

June: Sintra is cooler than Lisbon and can be misty; Adraga beach is dramatic but Atlantic-cold.

Save Choose Regaleira over a full Pena interior day; the tunnels and Initiation Well are more kid-magical and the pricing is clear (about EUR50 for the four of us).
Splurge Add Pena Palace only if you can secure an early timed slot; current rules require booking the exact day/time for palace entry.

Family & picky-kid eats

Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Pena Palace park/terraces ↩ — more iconic color, but stricter timed entry and more crowd pressure.
  • Monserrate Palace ↗ — lush gardens and lighter crowds than Pena if the main Sintra palaces are slammed.
  • Praia da Rainha ↗ — tiny Cascais cove for an easier swim/photo if the Sintra weather is poor.

Lisbon: Belém, Alfama & Tram 28

PhotosIG · LisbonIG · BelémFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

City wandering is free. Belém Tower and Jerónimos Monastery are timed-entry, roughly EUR8-15 adult each with reduced/free kids; Tram 28 is a normal transit ride (~EUR3) or on a 24h Viva Viagem pass.

Weather

June: Lisbon runs warm, about 77-84 F and hotter than the coast. Do Alfama and Belém earlier, then riverside gelato and shade in the afternoon.

Save Ride the real Tram 28 as your sightseeing instead of a paid hop-on bus, and use a reloadable Viva Viagem card for the family.
Splurge Pastéis de Belém from the original bakery, or the Lisbon Oceanarium if a kid needs an indoor reset.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Pena Palace park ↗ — colorful terraces and forested grounds when the full interior visit feels too crowded.
  • Praia das Macas ↗ — bigger family beach with food nearby if cliff coves feel too exposed.

Cabo da Roca, Praia da Ursa & Azenhas do Mar

PhotosIG · Cabo da RocaIG · Praia da UrsaFlickr
1 / 2
Cost

Free. Cabo da Roca (mainland Europe’s westernmost point) has free parking and viewpoints; Praia da Ursa is a steep unofficial scramble (not for little kids alone); Azenhas do Mar has a natural sea pool below the village.

Weather

June: sunny but windy and Atlantic-cold water. Great for views and tide pools, not long swims — that is what Sicily is for.

Save Pack a picnic — there is little food at Cabo da Roca beyond one café, and the views beat overpriced snacks.
Splurge A cliffside lunch or sunset drink at Azenhas do Mar, or a short Sintra-coast e-bike stretch.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Na Pizza Cascais — Margherita listed at EUR13; easy plain pizza night
  • Pasta Non Basta Cascais — fresh pasta and pizza, simple tomato/cheese fallback
  • Passione Pizza Sintra — Sintra pizza stop with plain kid-friendly orders
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Praia da Adraga / Guincho ↩ — easier sand-and-café beaches if the Ursa scramble is too much.
  • Praia das Macas ↗ — bigger family beach with food nearby if cliff coves feel too exposed.
  • Lisbon Oceanarium ↗ — reliable bad-weather or jet-lag backup before the Sicily flight.

SleepPalma · night 2 of 2

This is the Mallorca variety day: one Gothic city block and one Tramuntana mountain-port block. The old train is charming but expensive for four, so treat it as optional.

Palma old town + La Seu Cathedral

PhotosIG · palmaIG · laseuIG · palmademallorcaFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Palma old town and Parc de la Mar are free. Cathedral baseline visit is roughly EUR10 adult, with children 9 and under free; terraces are a bigger EUR25 adult buy-up and not for under-9s, so the 8-year-old skips that version.

Weather

Mid-June Palma: warm, dry, and usually upper 70s to low 80s F. City stone gets hot after lunch, so do cathedral/old town early and move to a hotel pool or Cala Major/Portixol if the kids fade.

Save Skip the terrace ticket with the 8-year-old; the outside, Parc de la Mar, old lanes, gelato, and a simple cathedral interior ticket are the better family value.
Splurge One polished tapas dinner in Palma works here because the next four nights are beach-apartment simple.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Family-restaurant shortlist — current Palma family lists surface pizza, Italian, and casual Mediterranean options
  • Can Joan de s Aigo — ensaimada, ice cream, hot chocolate; ideal picky-kid fallback
  • Pizza fallback — Palma has multiple easy pizzerias if tapas fails
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Bellver Castle if the kids need ramparts instead of shopping streets.
  • Portixol waterfront for bikes/scooters and a calmer dinner walk.
  • Cala Major for a first Mallorca swim if the hotel pool is not enough.

Port de Soller + Tramuntana villages

PhotosIG · sollerIG · portdesollerIG · tramuntanaFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Historic Soller train official fare signal: Palma-Soller return EUR30, or Palma-Port de Soller train+tram EUR40. For four, that is EUR120-EUR160 before lunch, so this is the optional splurge unless the kids love trains.

Weather

Tramuntana: cooler than Palma in the morning, hot by the port after lunch. Roads are gorgeous but narrow; motion-sick kids do better with fewer village stops.

Save Drive the loop instead of paying the full train+tram stack; park once in Soller or Port de Soller and keep the day short.
Splurge Do the wooden train/tram combo once if the transportation itself is the attraction; it is expensive but memorable and eliminates mountain-road driving for the day.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Port de Soller promenade — pizza, grilled fish, ice cream, and fries all within one waterfront walk
  • Soller market snacks — oranges, pastries, and simple sandwiches keep the train day flexible
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Valldemossa only if the kids are done after one mountain village.
  • Deia viewpoint stop without the parking battle in the village core.
  • Hotel/pool afternoon if Tramuntana roads are slow or hot.

This is a pure vacation day sitting exactly where the calendar wants it: federal holiday, warm Mediterranean water, and zero logistics.

Playa de Muro + Alcudia Bay

PhotosIG · playademuroIG · alcudiaIG · mallorcabeachFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Beach is free; budget EUR25-EUR45/day for two loungers + umbrella when shade matters. Apartment-first lodging near Playa de Muro/Port d Alcudia is the budget gate: target $225-$300/night, walk away above ~$325/night.

Weather

Sea: Mallorca is usually about 72-75 F by mid/late June around Palma and the bays. Playa de Muro/Alcudia are shallow, sandy, and family-friendly, so this is the swim act Madeira cannot provide.

Save Use a grocery breakfast, beach picnic, and apartment laundry. Four low-drama beach nights are cheaper than a full resort if you keep the lodging gate.
Splurge One beach-club shade day or short bay boat trip is worth it if heat spikes or the 8-year-old needs an easier win.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Port d Alcudia pizza list — Pizza Stellata / Don Vito style fallback if seafood loses
  • Alcudia family restaurants — Indian, tapas, burgers, and beach menus within a short drive
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Hidropark Alcudia as the heat-wave rescue day, not a default spend.
  • S Albufera boardwalk for a cheap nature hour near the beach.
  • Alcudia old town walls at golden hour when the beach wind picks up.

A scenery day without abandoning the beach base. Go early, take the view, then return to water before the afternoon gets crowded and hot.

Formentor cliffs + Alcudia old town

PhotosIG · formentorIG · alcudiaIG · pollencaFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Viewpoints and Alcudia old-town walk are free. Budget parking, beach chairs if using Formentor beach, and extra fuel; seasonal access rules can push families onto shuttle/bus segments, so re-check before driving.

Weather

North cape: sunny, exposed, windy. Go early for viewpoints, then retreat to Playa de Muro for the reliable swim block.

Save Treat Formentor as a half-day viewpoint loop, not an all-day remote beach mission with parking stress.
Splurge A short Pollenca/Alcudia dinner after the cape is a better spend than forcing every viewpoint in one hot afternoon.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Alcudia old town — tapas for adults, pizza/burgers nearby for kids
  • Port de Pollenca promenade — easy gelato and fries fallback after the cape road
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Mirador Es Colomer only if road restrictions or crowds are ugly.
  • Pollenca steps for a town + view alternative without cape traffic.
  • Stay on Playa de Muro if wind makes the exposed north unpleasant.
