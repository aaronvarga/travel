Portugal starts the trip with scenery, pastries, tiled streets, and an easy coast, not a hard city day.

Cascais waterfront + Boca do Inferno

PhotosIG · CascaisIG · Boca do InfernoFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Waterfront, beaches, and Boca do Inferno are free. Lisbon-Cascais train is roughly EUR2-3 each way; taxi/transfer from LIS to Cascais often lands around $65-$110.

Weather

June: about 73-76 F days / 59-64 F nights. Atlantic water is chilly, roughly 62-64 F, so treat it as scenery and quick dips, not the main swim act.

Save Stay in Cascais/Estoril and use train/taxi instead of renting a Portugal car for only two nights.
Splurge Private airport transfer to Cascais after the overnight flight; worth the ~$65-$110 if everyone is tired.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Na Pizza Cascais — Margherita listed at EUR13; easy plain pizza night
  • Pasta Non Basta Cascais — fresh pasta and pizza, simple tomato/cheese fallback
  • Passione Pizza Sintra — Sintra pizza stop with plain kid-friendly orders
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Lisbon Oceanarium ↩ — excellent bad-weather or jet-lag backup; current tickets are EUR25 adults/13+, EUR15 ages 3-12.
  • Monserrate Palace ↗ — lush gardens and lighter crowds than Pena; good if timed palace slots are gone.
  • Praia da Rainha ↗ — tiny in-town cove for a quick swim/photo when the open Atlantic is rough.

Sintra: Quinta da Regaleira + Praia da Adraga

PhotosIG · SintraIG · Quinta da RegaleiraIG · Praia da AdragaFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Quinta da Regaleira official 2026 pricing: EUR15 adult, EUR10 youth 6-17 and seniors 65+, under 6 free. There is no combined family ticket, so a family of 4 (two adults + two kids) is about EUR50. Pena Palace is timed-entry and more crowd-sensitive.

Weather

June: Sintra is cooler than Lisbon and can be misty; Adraga beach is dramatic but Atlantic-cold.

Save Choose Regaleira over a full Pena interior day; the tunnels and Initiation Well are more kid-magical and the pricing is clear (about EUR50 for the four of us).
Splurge Add Pena Palace only if you can secure an early timed slot; current rules require booking the exact day/time for palace entry.

Lisbon: Belém, Alfama & Tram 28

PhotosIG · LisbonIG · BelémFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

City wandering is free. Belém Tower and Jerónimos Monastery are timed-entry, roughly EUR8-15 adult each with reduced/free kids; Tram 28 is a normal transit ride (~EUR3) or on a 24h Viva Viagem pass.

Weather

June: Lisbon runs warm, about 77-84 F and hotter than the coast. Do Alfama and Belém earlier, then riverside gelato and shade in the afternoon.

Save Ride the real Tram 28 as your sightseeing instead of a paid hop-on bus, and use a reloadable Viva Viagem card for the family.
Splurge Pastéis de Belém from the original bakery, or the Lisbon Oceanarium if a kid needs an indoor reset.

Cabo da Roca, Praia da Ursa & Azenhas do Mar

PhotosIG · Cabo da RocaIG · Praia da UrsaFlickr
1 / 2
Cost

Free. Cabo da Roca (mainland Europe’s westernmost point) has free parking and viewpoints; Praia da Ursa is a steep unofficial scramble (not for little kids alone); Azenhas do Mar has a natural sea pool below the village.

Weather

June: sunny but windy and Atlantic-cold water. Great for views and tide pools, not long swims — that is what Sicily is for.

Save Pack a picnic — there is little food at Cabo da Roca beyond one café, and the views beat overpriced snacks.
Splurge A cliffside lunch or sunset drink at Azenhas do Mar, or a short Sintra-coast e-bike stretch.

Family & picky-kid eats

Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Praia da Adraga / Guincho ↩ — easier sand-and-café beaches if the Ursa scramble is too much.
  • Praia das Macas ↗ — bigger family beach with food nearby if cliff coves feel too exposed.
  • Lisbon Oceanarium ↗ — reliable bad-weather or jet-lag backup before the Sicily flight.

Chania old town + Nea Chora / Agii Apostoli

Cost

Harbor wandering and town beaches are free. Add $60-$150 family for chairs, gelato, simple dinner, and taxis/parking depending where you stay.

Weather

June: Chania averages around 84 F highs / 66 F lows; sea water around 73 F is the payoff the whole route is built around.

Save Make arrival and recovery days mostly free: harbor, beach, bakery breakfast, and a simple pizza/gyro dinner.

Balos Lagoon by Kissamos boat

PhotosIG · Balos LagoonFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Current 2026 pre-Jul-15 Gramvousa/Balos boat pricing: EUR 40 ages 13+, EUR 24 ages 3-12, plus EUR 1 municipal fee for passengers over 13. Family baseline about EUR 147 before meals.

Weather

Mid-June: hot, bright, and exposed. Water should be comfortable enough for real swimming, but shade and wind are the deciding factors.

Save Use the public Kissamos boat and bring snacks/water. Driving the rough Balos road adds car-risk and a steep walk back up.
Splurge Private boat only if the total trip is pricing near the low end; it can be wonderful, but it is the easiest budget blowout.

Imbros stays the family-accessible gorge: shorter, shadier, no ferry, and a good gut-check before deciding whether Samaria is a full descent or lazy-way outing on a later hiking day.

Imbros Gorge + south-coast swim

AllTrails
PhotosIG · ImbrosFlickrAllTrails
1 / 3
Cost

Entrance is typically EUR 3-EUR 5 pp cash. If self-driving, start at Imbros village and arrange a Komitades-to-Imbros return taxi/minibus before you begin; shared rides are commonly about EUR 8 pp, or roughly EUR 25-35 for a small private taxi.

June hike notes

8 km, 2.5-4 hrs: easier and shorter than Samaria, mostly downhill from Imbros village to Komitades with shade in the narrows. Still rocky underfoot, with no services inside the gorge; start early and carry water.

Save Use the self-drive pattern: park at Imbros, walk down to Komitades, then take the pre-arranged local taxi/minibus back to the car. No ferry is required, which is why this is the gentler family gorge.
Splurge Book a Chania transfer tour if you do not want to manage the car-return logistics; the hike itself stays the same but the day becomes simpler.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Oregano's — Agii Apostoli wood-fired pizza, fresh pasta, grilled meats, and Greek basics
  • Goody's Burger House — burgers, chicken burgers, fries, and junior menu fallback
  • Lithos Restaurant — beef burger with fries, grilled chicken, chicken/pork gyros, and skewers
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Samaria Gorge ↩ — the epic 16 km version if everyone is trail-tested and the heat forecast cooperates.
  • Frangokastello Beach ↗ — south-coast swim and castle views after the gorge or as a low-effort swap.
  • Limnoupolis Water Park ↗ — straight kid-win backup for heat, wind, or hike fatigue.

Epic all-day hike

Est. $230-$390 - park entry, buses/ferry, meals

RouteXyloskalo/Omalos -> Agia Roumeli, 16km descent
LogisticsEarly KTEL from Chania; ANENDYK ferry out; return bus from Sougia/Sfakia
SleepFinal Chania night

Samaria supplies a full mountain-to-sea day when the family wants the leg’s biggest hike. For the 8-year-old, the complete descent is conditional; the shorter route from Agia Roumeli toward the Iron Gates is the kid-friendlier substitute.

Samaria Gorge — full Lefka Ori descent

PhotosIG · Samaria GorgeIG · Lefka OriFlickr
1 / 3
Cost

Plan roughly EUR 65-EUR 100 family for park entry, KTEL buses, and ANENDYK ferry tickets if self-guided; guided transfers commonly price higher but simplify the bus/ferry chain. Bring cash for small add-ons in Agia Roumeli.

June heat

Mountain start, hot finish: Xyloskalo/Omalos starts around 1,200-1,250 m, but the lower gorge and Agia Roumeli can feel full-summer hot by afternoon. Start on the first practical Chania bus, carry 2L+ water per adult, hats, snacks, and real shoes.

Save Use public logistics if everyone is comfortable with the sequence: early KTEL Chania -> Xyloskalo/Omalos, hike to Agia Roumeli, ANENDYK ferry to Sougia or Chora Sfakion, then the waiting KTEL bus back to Chania.
Splurge Book a guided transfer day if you want one throat to choke for ferry timing, bus seats, and pickup. It does not make the 16 km easier; it only removes logistics friction.

Family & picky-kid eats

  • Xyloskalo Restaurant — trailhead breakfast/coffee with the gorge view before the descent.
  • Faragi, Agia Roumeli — post-hike taverna; fries, grilled meat, simple pasta/rice-style kid orders.
  • Goody's Burger House Chania — late, plain burger/fries fallback after the long return.
Also awesome instead / if you have time
  • Lazy way from Agia Roumeli ↩ — ferry to Agia Roumeli, walk about an hour / ~2 km up to the Iron Gates, then return to the beach and ferry. This is the kid-friendly Samaria version if the full descent is too much.
  • Agia Roumeli beach ↗ — if you finish early, swim/eat before the 17:30 ferry window.
  • Limnoupolis Water Park ↗ — the no-guilt bailout if heat, legs, or ferry logistics are wrong.
